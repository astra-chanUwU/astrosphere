AstroSphere is a personal archive made to stay readable, portable, and easy to keep alive.

The site is written in Markdown and MDX, stored in a repository, and rendered with Astro. The default is static and lightweight, with JavaScript added only when it materially improves an interaction. That keeps the pages quick to open and the content easy to move or edit.

The structure is deliberately simple: artifacts are the individual pieces, spheres gather related interests, and trails create a few hand-made ways through them. Media stays separate from the source repository when it is large, and credits, warnings, and source links are treated as part of the work rather than afterthoughts.

The design will keep changing as the collection does. A colophon is useful here because it records the choices behind the surface without pretending they are permanent.
