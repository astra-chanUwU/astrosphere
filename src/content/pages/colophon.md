AstroSphere started as an idea for a personal archive and became a place to keep the things I care about in one shape.

The site is written in Markdown and MDX, stored in a repository, and rendered as a mostly static website with Astro. I like websites that are quick to open, easy to read, and understandable enough to keep alive.

The structure is deliberately simple: artifacts are the individual pieces, spheres gather related interests, and trails create a few hand-made ways through them. The design will probably keep changing as the collection does.
