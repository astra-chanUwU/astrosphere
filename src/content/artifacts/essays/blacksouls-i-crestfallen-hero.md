# Crestfallen Hero

The Crestfallen Hero is a small but memorable piece of BLACKSOULS’ Souls-like vocabulary: a despondent figure who can be spoken to before the route turns hostile. The nine source images record that progression and his later appearances.

> **Content warning:** Content warning: the route includes despair, monster transformation, and combat imagery.

## A depressed hero in the forest

The Holy Forest, dialogue-option, and depression frames let the encounter breathe before any fight begins. They establish the hero’s defeated posture as character information rather than mere scenery.
