# Black Vanguard Lindamea

Black Vanguard Lindamea is an enemy in *BLACKSOULS* and one of the members of the Black Trial. With five or more sins, Lindamea can chase Grimm through parts of the map, hunting him for his evil deeds. The encounter begins as a roaming threat and grows into a recurring judgment: Lindamea returns in the first game’s Ending C, comes back stronger in *BLACKSOULS II*, and is also planned to appear in *DEAD RED HOOD*.

> **Spoiler warning:** This page covers the Black Trial, Ending C, Lindamea’s sequel rematch, and later series material. Mature route context is acknowledged without explicit scene detail.

## Appearances | BLACKSOULS I

Lindamea is fast and can often catch Grimm before he has time to escape. Early in the game, he can kill Grimm almost instantly. As Grimm grows stronger, the fight becomes more manageable, but Lindamea remains a high-pressure enemy with a large health pool and the ability to regenerate.

Defeating him awards 55,000 souls and many Black Souls. The reward is valuable, but the encounter’s larger purpose is judgment: Lindamea is not a normal wandering enemy, but a member of the Black Trial who appears because Grimm has accumulated enough sin.

### Forest of Sabbath

Under certain conditions, Lindamea teleports beside Grimm after the Nameless Demon—Leonard—is defeated during Baphomet’s questline. He briefly acknowledges that Grimm defeated the enemy before him, then disappears again.

### Ending C

During the ruined version of the Holy Forest in Ending C, Lindamea appears at the Gathering of Black Souls. Grimm faces him in a final battle while the world is ending around them.

Lindamea remains as determined as ever to punish the sinners in the collapsing world. The encounter makes his role literal: even at the edge of destruction, the Black Trial continues its work.

## Appearances | BLACKSOULS II

Lindamea is one of the characters who first appeared in *BLACKSOULS I* and returns to life in the sequel.

### Fighting Lindamea, again

In the White Garden, Grimm encounters a fog. Passing through it reveals a black presence, and Lindamea deliberately corners him before initiating the rematch. This version of Lindamea is much stronger than the first game’s enemy.

Lindamea’s first domain is Black Domain “Demon Wolf Who Judges.” It reduces Dark Resistance by 100% and Crit Evasion by 100%. His Hunting Horse also becomes an enemy during the encounter.

After Grimm defeats Lindamea once, Lindamea dismounts and summons creepy Guardian Eyes to help him. Defeating this phase triggers another regeneration and another domain. Black Domain “Trial by Blade” makes both combatants immortal and increases magic and attack power by 900%.

After enough time passes, Grimm can finally deal the finishing blow. Defeating Lindamea awards the Soul of the Outer One, Black Knight’s Ring, and many Black Souls.

After the fight, Lindamea acknowledges that Grimm has grown stronger. He asks whether Grimm is cooperating with Red Hood and orders him to tell her that Lindamea will hunt her without mercy if she continues to live as a sinner. He then says that he will rebuild the Black Trial anew and threatens Grimm never to forget him while he is in a Garden.

## Appearances | DEAD RED HOOD

Lindamea is also set to appear in *DEAD RED HOOD*, which the source guide describes as being in development. The available image presents him on horseback, extending the Black Vanguard’s visual language beyond the two BLACKSOULS games.
