# Witch Dorothy

Witch Dorothy is one of BLACKSOULS’ clearest mentors: stern, funny, and deeply wounded. Her gallery traces an apprenticeship through the Witch’s House, old pupils, the loss of Toto, and a return that makes BLACKSOULS II feel like a haunted continuation rather than a clean sequel.

> **Content warning:** Adults-only content warning: Dorothy’s route includes sexual content, coercion, captivity, grief, violence, and disturbing backstory material. This page provides context without explicit scene prose.

## A witch and her apprentice

The Witch’s House, Hansel and Gretel, fairytale inspection, training, and quest images establish Dorothy’s teaching role. Her sharp dialogue and flustered reactions preserve the comic surface that makes later grief land harder.
