# Resigned Man

Resigned Man is found in the Embryo’s Dream brothel in Rotten Burg. His brief dialogue is less a quest than an eyewitness account: he condemns the Burgomaster’s lust, describes the town’s corruption, and frames his own remaining time as an escape into the brothel’s comforts.

The encounter is easy to overlook, but its aftermath gives it weight. Once the Knight in Armor dies, the brothel’s residents die too; on return, Resigned Man is only bones. That state change turns an otherwise static bit of dialogue into a compact warning about how much of Rotten Burg is held together by the local boss.
