# Rich Bird Goose

Rich Bird Goose is a companion built around an impossible contradiction: she steals souls, yet insists that the theft is for people who have less. Her bright, birdlike design makes that idealism easy to trust; the route’s failures make its price impossible to ignore.

> **Content warning:** Adults-only content warning: Goose’s route includes nudity, sexual content, captivity, death, and coercive branches. This page preserves source screenshots for route documentation without reproducing explicit prose.

## A thief with a cause

The first meeting, release, tent, forest, and Elma scenes establish Goose’s social world. Her apparent innocence is part of the route’s tension: the player meets a companion who is already balancing care for strangers against the danger she creates.

## Design

Goose’s wings, white hair, red eyes, and feathered silhouette distinguish her from the game’s more conventionally equipped companions. The final image with Elma makes her look at home in a friendship the plot continually threatens.
