# Talking Flower

At the far north of the Forest of Abandonment, the Talking Flower offers 5,000 souls toward a Herb Flask and asks the player to use Anti-Fairy against the fairies that have been tormenting it. Killing the flower is possible but produces no comparable reward.

After all four Demonic Princesses are dead, the friendly flower becomes the Lord of Corruption. The transformation changes the gentle errand into a boss encounter and supplies the answer to its fixation on fairies. Its late dialogue implies an older grievance before ending abruptly.
