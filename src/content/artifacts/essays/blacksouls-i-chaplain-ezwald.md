# Black Chaplain Ezwald

Ezwald’s polite offers are structured like an early warning: every response makes the player weigh immediate help against the Black Trial’s larger threat. The guide’s dialogue sequence makes that tension legible before the Black Chaplain steps into view.

## Offer, warning, and Ending C

The article frames Ezwald as both a decision point and a recurring witness to the game’s late-route material. The Ending C and Gigantic Turnip images preserve the connections that can be missed when the route is treated as a single fight.
