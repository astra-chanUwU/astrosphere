Queen of the Heart Lorina is a character in BLACKSOULS 2. Lorina is the Queen of the Heart and one of the Four Nightmares in Wonderland. She’s based on the Queen of Hearts from Lewis Carroll’s book, “Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland”.

## Appearances | BS2

Lorina is inside her castle, Red Castle Frissel. It takes a bit of effort to get to her.

### Meeting Lorina

Lorina is located in the Queen’s Chamber area, which is inside the Red Castle Frissel. To get to her in the first place, go upstairs when you enter the castle, and then go to the right and just keep exploring the area until you reach her.

Once you’re in front of the fog that leads to her, there’s a chance you’ll be able to summon the Donkey King to help you fight her.

Once you pass through the fog, you will have to choose certain dialogue options to indicate why you came. Whatever you say doesn’t really matter, because she’ll just say “Off with his head”.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-queen-of-the-heart-lorina/fighting-lorina-s-knights.webp" alt="Fighting Lorina's knights" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Fighting Lorina's knights</figcaption></figure>

Three of her knights will corner you and initiate a fight with you.

The knights who will be fighting you are:

- Jude Kelt, the Knight of Spades

- Kiska, the Knight of Hearts

- Pin, the Knight of Clubs

Just like the rest of the enemies in the Red Castle Frissel, these knights aren’t too hard to overcome. Killing them will give you the Soul of the Knight of Spades, the Soul of the Knight of Hearts, and the Soul of the Knight of Clubs.

This will give you the chance to talk to Lorina, who will be very angry because her knights have been killed.

If you tell her you’re going to Alice, she’ll just laugh it off because she isn’t in this world. Lorina ordered Alice to be executed. She will then tell you to kill yourself and join Alice. If you decide to talk to her again, she’ll start talking about Griffy and how she will come and kill you.

### Covenant and Its Events

Lorina is one of the characters you can get in a covenant with.

- Level 0 – Cost: Free. Abilities: Fire/Lightning/Ice/Dark/Light Resistance+15%. Options: X.

- Level 1 – Cost: 10000. Abilities: Fire/Lightning/Ice/Dark/Light Resistance+25%. Options: Kiss, Buy Cards.

- Level 2 – Cost: 20000. Abilities: Fire/Lightning/Ice/Dark/Light Resistance+35%. Options: Kiss, I want you to service me, Buy Cards.

- Level 3 – Cost: 30000. Abilities: Fire/Lightning/Ice/Dark/Light Resistance+50%. Options: Kiss, I want you to service me, Make love, Buy Cards.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-queen-of-the-heart-lorina/lorina-s-covenant.webp" alt="Lorina's covenant" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina's covenant</figcaption></figure>

When you approach Lorina and ask to get in a covenant with her, she will at first think you are coming for her neck too, until she realizes you just want a covenant. Lorina is feisty – she will ask if you’ll be able to replace the fallen knights, so she orders you to bow and swear an oath of eternal allegiance to her.

After you form a covenant, she will tell you not to call her the Queen of the Heart.

When you’re in a covenant with Lorina and have the Queen’s Tarts in your inventory, the next time you talk to her she will call you out, saying how she made those tarts in secret and left them there, so it would be “very grand of you” to anticipate her desires and bring them over to her. If you give her the tarts, she will eat them happily, and ask you what you are staring at, and if you keep the tarts, she will shout “off with your head”.

Level 0 dialogue: “Wake up early for a beheading, enjoy a beheading with afternoon tea, and a beheading after dinner is a must! Did you understand? Quickly, bring me a neck!”

Miscellaneous interactions with Lorina.

### Corpse-Eaters

If you beat Meryphillia, you can go back and report to Lorina that you took care of her. Lorina doesn’t like corpse-eaters at all, so she usually sends them to the Slaughterhouse so they can be killed “forever” because it’s almost impossible for them to die.

Lorina says that they have a pathogen that makes them crave dead flesh and that despite that, they try to become “the living”. According to Lorina, it’s perhaps because “they yearn for the pure souls, though we wouldn’t know…”

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-queen-of-the-heart-lorina/griffy-and-the-influence-of-the-red-queen.webp" alt="Griffy and the influence of the Red Queen" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Griffy and the influence of the Red Queen</figcaption></figure>

She says that the main problem with corpse-eaters is that they can read the memories of humans they eat and disguise themselves after them. They can easily trick another human being. Lorina says that accepting their existence will “bring swift ruin to this country!”.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-queen-of-the-heart-lorina/endings-f-and-g-lorina-1.webp" alt="Endings F and G, Lorina, 1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Endings F and G, Lorina, 1</figcaption></figure>

If Griffy went with you to kill the corpse-eater, then Lorina would have a slightly different dialogue. If you defeat Meryphillia without Griffy, Lorina will give you a small reward of 3000 souls, and each one of every colored soul.

### Lorina’s Assassination

At the end of Griffy’s storyline, she will come to the Queen’s Chamber and assassinate Queen Lorina. When you come to see Lorina, you will find Griffy on her throne, who is under a lot of pressure as she killed the Queen of Hearts, who was begging and crying to her very death.

It seems that Lorina didn’t fight back while Griffy was trying to kill her.

When you come into the room, there’ll be a lot of blood on Lorina’s throne.

Griffy will say that the “mad bloodline must be destroyed to the last. Just like the Red Queen said… Hahaha, hihi……”. The Red Queen that Griffy mentions is Prickett, but after that, all that’s left is to fight the demonbeast version of Griffy.

Although nothing of this has to happen if you don’t care about Griffy’s storyline, or if you decide to kill her along the way.

### True Identity

The Queen of the Heart Lorina probably used to be Lorina Liddell at some point – the girl you meet in the visual novel section of the game that’s placed way later in DLC3. Lorina Liddell is based on real-life Alice’s older sister, who went by the same full name.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-queen-of-the-heart-lorina/lorina-sex-on-her-throne.webp" alt="Lorina sex on her throne" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina sex on her throne</figcaption></figure>

Even though Lorina looks slightly different than she did before, she will often display open jealousy towards Alice and will even call you “teacher” when you try to kill her.

Lorina is jealous that Lewis Carroll chose Alice, her younger sister, over her. He used to be a teacher, and that’s why Lorina calls him by that name, since it’s the only one she’s used to calling him.

In the Mental Ward, there’s a rabbit doll that says “Liddell’s Doll” when you interact with it. During the H scenes with Lorina Liddell, she will be holding that doll with her, so even though Edith is the one stuck in the Mental Ward, it’s possible that Lorina was there for some time as well.

If you have 0 SEN, the sign at the entrance area of the Red Castle Frissel says: “I won’t allow her to hog the teacher to herself. I’ll get him to care about me, even if I have to offer my soul to an evil God.”

## Personality

Lorina is quite immature, and that’s very visible in her behavior. This also makes her very cute as she appears very innocent and at times whimsical. She’s very possessive when you get into a covenant with her, she will find it very hard to let go of you. Lorina loves the player a lot.

## Design

Lorina’s design is very unique and distinguishable. She has short black hair, pale skin, and dark red eyes. Her body is tiny and she’s very small in comparison to some of the stuff she’s wearing. For example, her black cape is really big and even has some kind of spikes attached to certain parts of it.

Under her cape, she has a red dress and, on her legs, she has black thigh highs.
