Mad Bird Jubjub is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 you can find at the top of the Sick Clock Tower, in the Jubjub’s Nest area. She’s a mad bird who likes to spread madness all across Wonderland, she’s also addressed by Node as one of the Four Nightmares.

She’s based on the Jubjub bird mentioned in works written by Lewis Carroll – the poems “Jabberwocky” and “The Hunting of the Snark“.

## Appearances | BS 2

Jubjub makes important appearances in the second game and she plays a crucial role in its story.

### Meeting Jubjub

Getting to Jubjub takes some time as she’s located on the top of the Sick Clock Tower – she has her own nest there.

To get to Jubjub’s Nest, you will first have to travel through the entire Sick Clock Tower to reach the top of it. Go upstairs and pass the fog if you want to meet Jubjub. You can get to these areas after you reach Ox Ward University and go through it.

Jubjub’s Nest will at first be empty, but then she’ll fly towards you.

If you tell her you’re going to Alice, she’ll just tell you to get naked and then attack you.

### Fighting the Mad Bird Jub-Jub

Jubjub has her own unique model during the fight, where you can see her just as you would outside the fight.

Before the fight, and before reaching Jubjub’s Nest, you can summon Peter Pan to help you fight her. But when you get to the fight, he will betray you, and he will be on her side, fighting you – Jubjub is a very attractive woman, I can understand him.

Jubjub has a ton of health, but she isn’t hard to beat at all, defeating her will give you a Tainted Black Soul, and an Ore Slab.

### Defeated Jubjub

When you defeat Jubjub in a fight you will be presented with a few options, before killing her for real. You can either enter a covenant with her, kill her, or force her to an intercourse with you.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-mad-bird-jubjub/jubjub-spare-or-kill.webp" alt="Jubjub, spare or kill" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Jubjub, spare or kill</figcaption></figure>

Forming a covenant will Jubjub will not prevent her from killing most of the NPCs in the game after a certain level of progression. Only killing her, or sending her to the dungeon, will prevent her and everyone else from going mad in the game.

Killing her will give you the Soul of the Jubjub, and the Gear of Madness.

The Gear of Madness is one of the most useful items in the game because with it you can change your SEN whenever you want.

### Covenant

Jubjub is one of the characters in the game you can form a covenant with. The covenant will increase your EVA&MEV stats, along with the chances for Magic Reflect. The final level of the covenant increases both statistics up to 30%. Her covenant is one of the more expensive ones.

When you reach a certain level within her covenant, Jubjub will be willing to sing for you if you want to. You will have a selection of songs you can choose her to sing, and all songs are rather morbid.

### Jubjub’s Madness

Jubjub’s Madness is an event that happens when the game reaches 10 Progression Points, if Jubjub is still alive and not in a dungeon. When the conditions are met Jubjub will cause madness throughout the entirety of Wonderland – she will kill people, make people kill themselves, and do other very morbid and grotesque things.

Everyone will go mad and if you suddenly lose one of your covenants out of nowhere, then it’s probably because you hit 10 Progression Points and Jubjub is still out there.

### Fighting the Bird of Cosmos

If you have 0 SEN then the fight with Jubjub will look different. She will be in her nightmare form, fighting you as the Bird of Cosmos. She’s stronger than regular Mad Bird Jub-Jub so you should prepare yourself if you’re looking to fight that version of her – the amount of souls you get from her is significantly higher.

### Little Sister Alice

Jubjub is the real identity of Little Sister Alice. She’s one of the Three Alices you have to choose at the beginning of the cycle, and out of the three, she’s the one who cares about you the most.

Little Sister Alice is tired of Wonderland and the never-ending cycles. During Ending H she’s the one who stops Mother Alice and Daughter Alice from attacking you, letting you escape so you can find the real Alice waiting for you. This will make them stop for a bit, which leads to a moment where the Three Alices’ true identities are shown.

In Winterbell you can find a flashback of an argument Mabel and Little Sister Alice had – it ends with Little Sister Alice not being able to deny she fell in love with her older brother, and she then proceeds to insult Mabel. Her language is often quite vulgar.
