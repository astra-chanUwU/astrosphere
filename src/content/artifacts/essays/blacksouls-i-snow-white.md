# Snow White

Snow White’s fairytale imagery is bent into a possessive, mirror-led ruler in *BLACKSOULS I*. The dwarfs, her self-addressed dialogue, and the covenant conversation all establish the contrast between a familiar tale and its hostile BLACKSOULS interpretation.
