# Dissolution Queen Sho

> **Adults-only notice:** This guide may discuss explicit scenes, coercion, violence, and spoilers.

Dissolution Queen Sho is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 you can find at the Mountains of Madness in the DLC3 part of the game. Sho is a shoggoth – they are amorphous creatures from the Cthulhu Mythos.


## Appearances | BS2


Sho is one of the characters in the second game who you can only find in the DLC3 part of the game.


### Mountains of Madness


You can find Sho for the first time inside the Mountains of Madness if you go down the area where Gerda was spacing out.


Sho will be very happy to see you because it means that now she can multiply. She’s cheery and friendly, so you won’t get in any trouble when you meet her.


You can do plenty of things to her, and even form a covenant. Multiplying will initiate an NSFW scene where she’ll also drain all of your health.

### Melted Gerda


While going through the Mountains of Madness you will encounter Gerda right before the path that leads to Sho. Gerda will be spaced out, saying how she can hear crickets and how someone is calling her.


If you leave her there without bringing her back to her senses, she will wander away towards Sho and Sho will melt her.


Sho wanted to be friends with Gerda, but Gerda was so scared of Sho that she cried, and then Sho melted her because she was annoying. She didn’t even shoot her gun.

### Covenant


Sho is one of the characters you can form a covenant with. Her covenant is one of the more expensive ones though, but it will reduce the amount of damage you take, and it will also reduce your recovery percentage.


While leveling up her covenant she will say how she was also given a role to play, but then she couldn’t adapt to it and she melted.


If you ask Sho to service you, she will gladly do it and even mention how Mary Sue said that Grimm is a “fun one”. At the end of the NSFW scene, she will start melting you, and unfortunately, melt your male reproductive organ as well.


### Fighting the Dissolution Queen Sho


When you meet Sho you can instantly start a fight with her if you want to kill her. She may seem like an easy enemy to defeat at first, but once she reaches her second phase she will activate her domain and summon a couple of Slimes to help her in the fight.


Her domain is Dissolving Domain “Meltdown”, which reduces your Max HP by -90%, and your Critical chance by -100%.


By killing her you will get a Soul of the Outer One and plenty of other colored souls.


## Personality


Sho is very jealous of Alice, she doesn’t like her at all. But despite that jealousy, Sho has a very nice and friendly personality. She can’t control herself sometimes and likes to melt things, but other than that she’s very nice to be around.


## Design


Sho isn’t human and she doesn’t look like one either, if you disregard her humanoid shape. She’s a slimy being with the form of a beautiful curvy woman. Her hair is very long, and she seems to have some kind of cat or dog ears. Her pupils are bright green, while the rest of her eyes are black.
