# Drake Helkaiser

Drake Helkaiser is the dragon rival encountered at Fort Ivern, then in progressively ruined forms at Great Wilhelm Bridge and Tainted Bog. FGGuides follows his return through *BLACKSOULS II* and the Chaos Dungeon; the complete source gallery is preserved below in article order.
