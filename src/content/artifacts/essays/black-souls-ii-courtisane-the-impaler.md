# Courtisane the Impaler

> **Adults-only notice:** This guide may discuss explicit scenes, coercion, violence, and spoilers.

Courtisane the Impaler is a character in *BLACKSOULS II* who appears in DLC3. She is Lingeriena’s and Victoria’s sister, an Impaler during the events at Crimean Nursing Graveyard, and a demon of violence like both of them.


She’s one of the characters who will be appearing in Dead Red Hood, where she’s a Princess of some sort.


## Appearances | BS2


Courtisane has a brief appearance in the second game in DLC3, in the Crimean Nursing Graveyard.


### Saving Lingeriena


After defeating Lingeriena and completing the intimate scene with her, Grimm attempts to kill her. Courtisane intervenes by driving a spear through Grimm’s brain, saving her sister from death.


Courtisane attacked Grimm to save her sister. She will also scold Lingeriena for getting herself into such a dangerous situation.


Courtisane asks whether Grimm is still alive. Lingeriena immediately claims that he is dead, preventing a fight between Courtisane and Grimm from starting.


## Appearances | DRH


In 2023 Toro made a post on X/Twitter that’s a teaser for Dead Red Hood. He labeled Courtisane as a new character who will appear in DRH, with an image of her attached to the tweet.


Toro’s teaser presents Courtisane as a new *Dead Red Hood* character with a princess-like role, expanding her story beyond the brief DLC3 appearance.
