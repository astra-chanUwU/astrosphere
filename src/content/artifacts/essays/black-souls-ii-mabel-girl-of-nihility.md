# Mabel, Girl of Nihility

- BS2

- DLC3

- Entities

- Great Ones

- Guide

Mabel, Girl of Nihility, is an important character in BLACKSOULS 2 that’s introduced to the game in the Old King of Chess DLC. You meet her for the first time in the Chaos Dungeon, which is an area where you can always find her, it’s a part of her garden – Shatranj.

She has a significant role later in the game during DLC3, as she’s one of the Great Ones.

## Appearances | BS2

Mabel is an important character in the BLACKSOULS series. She makes her official appearance in BS2 and can be encountered in the early part of the game.

### Chaos Dungeon

Mabel appears for the first time in the Chaos Dungeon area which is accessed through a door you can find in the Library Dream’s Dungeon area. Once you pass through the door you’ll come to a unique place that’s a part of Mabel’s garden.

At first, you won’t see Mabel here, until you try to go through one of the doors. She’ll then pop up and introduce herself to you and immediately declare how Alice isn’t there, describing the chaotic nature of the new place you can dwell in.

If you don’t see Mabel in the Chaos Dungeon that’s because you need to reach the 1st layer of the Chaos Dungeon for her to appear.

When Mabel is in the Chaos Dunegon’s main area you can talk to her and she will have plenty of random dialogue you can exhaust. You can’t send her to the dungeon and you can’t kill her, she will just make fun of you whenever you do that.

Further down in the Chaos Dungeon, Mabel will accompany you through various different parts of it. The Chaos Dungeon is the best place in the game for farming valuable items and souls, the more you progress through it the harder it gets and the rewards get better.

While going through its layers, Mabel will wait for you at every checkpoint located right behind the fog of a boss you have to fight. You will be able to heal yourself, level up, buy colored souls, and even reinforce your weapons. She’s of significant help, and she’s also very cute.

The Chaos Dungeon has the special Ashes Reingnited mode that is different from regular Chaos Dungeon progression through layers. Here you progress through specific boss fights and at the end of the Ashes Reignited, the last enemy you fight will be Mabel herself.

Mabel is a very strong enemy and she can summon a few powerful allies to help her out during certain moments in the fight. Mabel can summon the God of Sleep, the Lake Inhabiting God, and the Black Goat That Births Madness.

She will then go on to mention the Crown who you know under the name of Grand Guignol, and how it’s making good use of this land.

### Final Moments

When you reach The Throne area after traveling through the entirety of Winterbell that was available to you, Mabel will be waiting for you at the staircase towards the Grand Guignol.

Mabel calls this way of thinking boring and compares it to how it’s impossible to create purity in a tainted soul because eventually, you will break.

She connects that with why Grand Guignol needs Grimm because, with the ability to create and the ability to innovate, it will be possible to make some of the “most wonderful of Gardens together”.

Mabel will then realize she has been talking for too long and will go towards the stairway, telling you to hold her hand as she will guide you to Grand Guignol.

## Personality

As a character, Mabel is very loose. She’s obsessed with indulging in sinful acts, such as drinking and other types of slothfulness. Mabel often refers to herself in conversation as “frail”, “poor”, “weak”, and etc.

I think she’s pretty funny and I enjoyed her storyline the most in the game. I like that she’s an alcoholic, and I think many can relate to her problems with addiction. It’s touching how much of an emotional impact Grimm has on Mabel, especially by the end of the DLC3.

## Design

Mabel has a very distinguishable color palette – blue, red, and black which kinda makes a darker version of Alice in terms of appearance.

After all, she’s based on Mabel from Lewis Carroll’s Alice in Wonderland who’s used as a bad example in the book. Mabel has long dark blue hair with a red ribbon on the top of her head.

She’s always wearing a gothic Lolita dress with red borders that look puffy. Mabel has black thigh highs and very nice thighs. Her eyes are one of the best parts of her design – if you look closer you can even see the stars in them. They perfectly fit her pale shy face that blushes quite frequently.
