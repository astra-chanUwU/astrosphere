# Prostitute Marianna

Marianna—who asks to be called Mary—is a small but unusually painful *BLACKSOULS I* route in Rotten Burg. She works in Embryo’s Dream, accepts a night for souls, and refuses to abandon the city where she was born even when the fog makes that loyalty dangerous.

> **Adults-only warning:** Marianna’s route involves prostitution, sexual encounters, transformation, and body horror. The gallery is retained as route documentation, without explicit prose.

## A conditional reprieve

The game makes Marianna’s survival a chain of uncomfortable conditions. Spending a night with her and giving her souls can delay her transformation after the Knight in Armor is defeated; without that, she becomes a vampire and fights amid unborn children. Later, even the apparent reprieve becomes temporary as Rotten Burg collapses around her.

## The Basement is not rescue

The guide identifies sending Marianna to the Basement as the only reliable way to save her from transformation. That is a classic *BLACKSOULS* bargain: mechanical safety purchased through a setting whose sexualized control makes “saving” morally unstable. Marianna’s gentle tone and attachment to Rotten Burg stop the decision from reading as a neutral optimization.

Her Harlot’s Ring, dropped if she dies, gives bonus-action utility. The item is useful, but the route’s point is that usefulness sits beside a human choice the player has been invited to instrumentalize.
