# Shaman Keto

Shaman Keto occupies a cave in the north of the Skeleton Maze. Too much fog traversal advances the world past him, leaving only a pile of bones, so meet him before pushing the maze’s progression state.

His route utility is unusually high for a small merchant: with Jeanne’s ashes and a Kraken’s Tentacle, he enables her revival, the prerequisite for obtaining her fairytale. He also sells spells, making the cave a useful stop for a magic-oriented run.
