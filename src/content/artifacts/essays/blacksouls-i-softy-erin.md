# Softy Erin

Erin is a talking mushroom near the Entrance into Darkness after the Lust Demon Satyr. He blends into the scenery, so pause before leaving the area. His small conversation mentions the Black Trial and Oentius, while killing him only yields a modest number of souls and Erin’s Remedy.

That remedy completely restores HP and MP, making it much more memorable than the kill reward. Preserve the encounter if the consumable or the dialogue matters to the run.
