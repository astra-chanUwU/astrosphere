# Dying Warrior

Dying Warrior is found beside the bonfire in Jacob’s Disused Mine, poisoned by a Toxic Butterfly. Giving him one Antidote Herb and one Medical Herb completes his request and earns three Ore Chunks, three Large Ore Chunks, and three Ore Shards.

Killing him yields the ore alongside a Soul of a Nameless Soldier, but also incurs sin. The screenshots document both outcomes; the route therefore makes a simple resource decision part of the game’s larger moral economy.
