# Living with Red Hood

*Living with Red Hood* is reportedly a short adventure game planned as an extra for *DEAD RED HOOD*. Its premise is deliberately more intimate than the main game: Red Hood and the protagonist of *BLACKSOULS* live together, sharing a home rather than moving from mission to mission through a horror-filled world.

## A softer companion to DEAD RED HOOD

The available description presents this as a short ADV rather than another stealth-action game. Red Hood and the *BLACKSOULS* protagonist are together in a domestic setting, and the focus is meant to be their time living together.

That makes the extra feel like a tonal counterpoint to *DEAD RED HOOD*. The main game is built around the Black Trial, sinners, assassinations, and escape routes. *Living with Red Hood* is described as a space where the characters can simply stay together.

## Adult content without the horror framing

The companion is also described as containing extensive sexual content. In the supplied material, the point is that the player can have sex with Red Hood freely, without the horror elements that define the main title. As with the rest of this project's adult content, the game is intended for adults only.

The contrast is part of the appeal: the same Red Hood who appears as a dangerous executioner in the main game is placed into a private, domestic scenario. Instead of stealth, targets, and the Black Trial's machinery, the extra promises a more direct fantasy of living with her.

## Still only a reported extra

This should be read as development information rather than a final feature list. *DEAD RED HOOD* is still upcoming, and the exact contents, release arrangement, and translation status of its companion material may change.

For now, *Living with Red Hood* sounds like a small, deliberately indulgent add-on: a no-horror ADV built around Red Hood, the *BLACKSOULS* protagonist, domestic life, and a great deal of adult intimacy.
