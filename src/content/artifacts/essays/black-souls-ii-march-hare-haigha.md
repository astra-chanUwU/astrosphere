# March Hare Haigha

March Hare Haigha is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 you can find at the Endless Tea Party. She’s a promiscuous rabbit, participating in a tea party between Sleeping Rat Dormouse and Hatter Hatta.

Haigha is based on the March Hare with the same name from Lewis Carroll’s books – “Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland” and “Through the Looking-Glass”.

## Appearances | BS2

Haigha appears for the first time in the second BLACKSOULS game. You can find her at the Endless Tea Party area that you can get to after going through Ox Ward University.

### Endless Tea Party

She’s at the Endless Tea Party with Hatta and Dormouse. This scene is an imitation of the Mad Tea Party, and the layout is almost exactly the same as the Finished Tea Party from the first game, although without Alice’s Library.

When you talk to Haigha and tell her that you’re going to Alice, she’ll tell you that you should have intercourse with her instead of with “that girl”.

Hatta will confront her because she’s a deviant, and then Haigha will start saying profanities but she will also censor them this time, which will make Hatta approve her actions.

If you try to throw Haigha into the dungeon, she will be very happy that you’re taking an interest in her. She’ll be very happy you decided to give her some love.

Also, if you don’t want to talk to any of the tea partygoers and just ignore them, Haigha will ask Hatta if someone just went past them, but Hatta will deny that anyone is there.

### Covenant with Hatta

While you may not form a covenant with Haigha, you can form one with Hatta, and leveling it up will also impact Haigha.

After you talk to Hatta for a little bit after fully leveling up the covenant, Haigha will start jumping in her seat, and she’ll be very excited about something. You can either check her stats, which are quite morbid, but you can also copulate with her, which brings a very nice NSFW scene.

### Fighting Haigha the March Rabbit

If your SEN is low, you will see Haigha in her creepy monster form. You won’t be able to talk to her or the rest of the attendees of the Endless Tea Party, because they’ll be speaking gibberish.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-march-hare-haigha/fighting-haigha-the-march-rabbit.webp" alt="Fighting Haigha the March Rabbit" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Fighting Haigha the March Rabbit</figcaption></figure>

You will have the option to kill her, and if you do a fight will initiate. When the fight happens, the rest of the tea party members will come to fight too.

### DLC3 Appearance

Haigha appears in the DLC3 part of the game along with Hatta. They will be sitting on a bench near a bonfire in the White Castletown area. They’re hard to miss really. Dormouse is missing from their group this time though.

Haigha will talk about how she’s freezing, which is quite understandable considering she’s almost naked in a land full of snow and ice. Hatta will be acting like her regular self too.

## Personality

Haigha is very eccentric, and if she had a talk with a psychologist, he’d give her nymphomania as a diagnosis. She’s always in heat, and ready to copulate but also very happy to do so. Other than that, she’s very vulgar, but her constant cheerfulness makes her nice.

## Design

Haigha is a physically well-developed woman and very attractive. She has long pink hair, pink eyes, and bunny ears. She’s an anthropomorphic hare, and she’s always in heat, so that’s why she doesn’t wear much clothing.
