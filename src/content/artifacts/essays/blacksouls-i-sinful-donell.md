# Sinful Donell

At the southwest edge of the Tainted Bog, Donell sells Soul of Sinner for 1,000 souls each. The purchase raises the sin counter, making him a straightforward way to tune sin-based equipment such as the Shadowmoon Blade.

Killing him gives a sin and 1,500 souls, but ends the shop. For any route that needs several sins, the merchant is more valuable alive than the one-time kill reward.
