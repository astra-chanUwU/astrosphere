# White Unicorn Unis

White Unicorn Unis October 2, 2024 By Nate River BLACKSOULS BS2 DLC3 Entities Guide Updated: February 4, 2025 Home BLACKSOULS White Unicorn Unis White Unicorn Unis is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 who’s the leader of the Order of the Pure Horn under the direct command of the White Queen. She’s a character you will encounter for the first time in the DLC3 part of the game. Contents 1 Appearances | BS2 1.1 Joining Unis 1.2 Kill Unis 1.3 Suspension Bridge 1.4 At the White Gate 1.5 Final Duel 1.6 Betrayal 2 Personality 3 Design Appearances | BS2 Unis is one of the characters that exclusively appears in the DLC3 part of the second game. She’s very important and you can either side with her, the Unicorn, or Leiden, the Lion, in the fight for the crown. Joining Unis You can meet Unis for the first time in the Unicorn’s Forest. That area is below the Avalanche Woods. You can come up to Unis and talk to her, she’ll be surprised that you’re male but if you tell her your name she will properly introduce herself to you. She’s the leader of the Order of the Pure Horn, and the White Queen is the one who commands her what to do. Joining Unis 1 of 2 Unis will welcome you to Winterbell. The reason she’s surprised that you’re male is because there are barely any in Winterbell, and there’s especially a low number of males that are like Grimm . She will instantly offer you to join her. Her rival is Leiden, the Lion, who’s trying to snatch away the crown from the Unicorn, i.e. Unis. When you join Unis, you can ask her what’s her goal. She’ll start talking about a savage clan – the Ravenous Fang Troop, and how it ran rampant through Winterbell. Allegedly they kill a lot of weak people. So today, the boss of the Ravenous Fang, White Lion Leiden, announced how she wants to usurp the White Castle, and they already started attacking. Then Unis will tell you to follow her, and she will disappear. Kill Unis Right when you meet Unis for the first time in the Unicorn’s Forest, you can kill her or try to send her to the dungeon. This way you will start a fight with Unis, and she will summon her Unseen Horses to help her out. While fighting Unis her appearance changes a little bit – she literally has a unicorn head. She will be very offended that a male tried to kill her just now, stating how she will pierce the filthy bigots with her horn of justice. Kill Unis 1 of 3 Unis will also activate a domain – White Domain “Unseen Unicorn”, which makes unavoidable attacks and skills nullified, and buffs disabled. Her horses alone are very strong, so you should really prepare yourself if you’re looking to defeat her. Taking all of her health will spawn three illusions of her. If you defeat Unis you will get the Soul of the White Unicorn, a Soul of the Outer One, 2 Tainted Black Souls, and a bunch of other colorful souls. If you chose the kill option to take her out, she’ll be surprised you weren’t after her body. Suspension Bridge When you meet Unis again, at the Suspension Bridge, she will be glad that you were able to catch up to her. She will then state how she thought you only wanted to join the alliance with her because of achieving physical contact with her. Suspension Bridge 1 of 2 But she will say that it looks like she’s mistaken. Although, only the Queen can recognize you as one of her knights. Unis will head off with her unicorns to the castle, passing the bridge At the White Gate After you meet Unis at the Suspension Bridge and talk to her, you will proceed further with her towards the castle until she stops, calling out Leiden who’s standing on top of the gate. At the White Gate 1 of 3 Unis and Leiden will argue with each other. Unis will get mad and send her unicorns charging through the gate but Leiden will eliminate them all while making huge holes in the ground which make it impossible to go through the gate. Final Duel After exploring White Castletown, you will eventually get to the aftermath of a deadly fight with blood and destruction present everywhere, and that will lead you to the Dueling Grounds area. There will be an ice bridge you have to cross and a fog through which you have to pass. Once you do that, you will see Leiden and she will talk to you for a little bit. But Unis will come soon enough and interrupt her, telling Leiden how she’s ready to end her tyranny. After that soon enough a fight will start, after Leiden gets angry and attacks first. Final Duel 1 of 2 If you manage to beat Leiden in a fight, Unis will say how she’s going to finish this and then she will proceed to step further. Leiden will be surprised that she lost, and she will ask Unis to kill her instead of begging for her life. Unis will kill her, saying how peace should be restored in Winterbell now, but you will get the option to either obey her or ask for a reward. If you ask her for a reward, she’ll think you’re asking to be granted a knighthood, but she’ll be quite surprised when you tell her you want sexual relief instead. She probably has one of the best NSFW scenes in the game. Final Duel, 2 1 of 2 Betrayal After you cross the bridge and finally leave the Dueling Grounds, Unis will lead you to the Queen’s Chamber, but then she will suddenly perform a surprise attack on you from behind, which you will skillfully dodge. She will tell you then how she will make sure to not miss next time, and how you were only a tool for her, and never an ally. Unis will call you a penis with legs walking around. Then Unis will proceed to have a mental breakdown, insulting males and everything related to them. She doesn’t like sexual acts and wants to “re-educate” all males. Unis wants to make a country for females by females. Betrayal 1 of 5 If you ask her what happened to equality, she’ll tell you to come closer and then she will start screaming pretending you’re going to assault her. She will then proceed to be very mean and say a lot of many other ill-willed things, towards you, and many other males. Then, when her breakdown reaches its climax, one of the unicorns will start speaking and they will all die in an instant. This will reveal that Mabel is actually a horse, or at least was one of the horses as she will come out of it directly and turn back to being a human again, making fun of you again. Betrayal, 2 1 of 4 Unis doesn’t know who even is Mabel, but she will insult her clothing saying how it’s made to attract males. Unis will get very agitated at Mabel’s response and she will start jumping, while also calling her an “exemplary pathetic whore!”. Then Unis will proceed to give Mabel a 5-second countdown to disappear, otherwise she’ll make her disappear, and then Mabel will knock her out and almost kill her. When you go and talk to Unis, you will notice how she’s not dead and only just unconscious, while muttering something in her sleep. She will be reminiscing about how she saw her parents have sex for the first time, and then she set her entire home on fire because it was really disgusting. You can wake her up, or just leave her be. If you wake her up she will just lash at you again, calling you an “ugly rotten dick on legs”. Poor Unis 1 of 2 She will continue with insults such as “return your penis and balls to God at once”. You can then either kill her, send her to the dungeon, or just ignore her. Personality One of Unis’ remarkable traits is that she hates males, when you try to send her to the dungeon, she’ll even admit that she was prosecuting and killing males so their population in Winterbell wouldn’t even reach more than 1%. This is most likely due to the trauma she experienced as a child when she saw her parents having sex, so she had to burn her house with them in it to feel better. She finds males and sexual acts disgusting. Design Unis is human but she's also part unicorn, and it's easy to notice because she has a large yellow unicorn horn sticking out of her head. Unis dresses in all white &#8211; her clothing covers her body fully, it's stylish and girly, but also very elegant. She has long white hair. Unis also wears glasses, and her eyes are purple, which goes nicely with her white and yellow color palette. Her facial expressions are either smug or mean. Her face alone is quite attractive.
