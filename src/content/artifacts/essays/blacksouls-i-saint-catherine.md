# Saint Catherine

Saint Catherine carries the language of kindness and faith into a world that repeatedly tests both. Her route connects the Holy Forest, Skeleton Maze, Cathedral, and later spirit material to a companion whose tenderness never makes Elysium safe.

> **Content warning:** Adults-only content warning: Catherine’s route includes sexual content, coercive branches, captivity, violence, and character death. The page documents the source gallery without explicit prose.

## Faith under pressure

The Maiden of Salvation encounter and early forest dialogue frame Catherine as a companion who responds to danger with devotion rather than certainty. The Skeleton Maze and Cathedral sequences show that faith as a practice, not a shield.
