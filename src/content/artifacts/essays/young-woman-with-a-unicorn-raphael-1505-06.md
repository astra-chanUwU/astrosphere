# Young Woman with a Unicorn

Raphael’s *Young Woman with a Unicorn* is calm in the way a locked door is calm. The sitter looks directly at us, composed and almost solemn, while the small unicorn rests in her arms. Behind her, the blue landscape opens into distance, but the portrait stays close: face, hands, fabric, and the animal held against her.

The unicorn traditionally suggests chastity, yet the symbol does not explain the woman away. Her expression remains private, and the animal feels less like an emblem than a strange companion. Raphael balances ideal beauty with a person who seems unwilling to perform her thoughts for the viewer.

Look at the contrast between the vivid red sleeves, the pale unicorn, and the cool landscape. Everything is clear, but nothing is fully disclosed. The painting’s mystery lives in that restraint: a young woman, a mythical animal, and a gaze that gives us no easy key.
