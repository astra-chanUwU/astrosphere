# Prayer Master

The Prayer Master is the black rabbit in the Holy Forest and the game’s essential level-up service. His menu also grows into a shop as progress advances. That practical role makes the violent option unusually consequential: killing him permanently removes the ordinary way to gain levels, even though the victory grants the Traitorous Rabbit’s Ring and its extra action.

## Holy Forest and Ending C

The fight is deliberately punishing early on. If you mean to preserve a normal progression route, leave the rabbit alive; the screenshots below document the dialogue, confrontation, and its reward context. In Ending C, after the completed fairytale route reaches the original Holy Forest, he explains his part in the older Alice-and-Grimm tragedy and his regret at becoming one of Mary Sue’s instruments.
