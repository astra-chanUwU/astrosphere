# Witch Glinda

Glinda is the “good witch” of *BLACKSOULS*, adapted from L. Frank Baum but remembered mostly through absence. She once mediated Dorothy’s long conflict with the Sabbath and Leonard. By the time the player reaches the Forest of Sabbath, that work has been erased by the Black Trial.

## A disaster written in a diary

After the Nameless Demon is defeated, the guide places Glinda’s apprenticeship diary on the bookshelves beyond the fight. It starts as a record of witches dwindling until only Glinda and Leonard remain. Then its writing breaks into panic: Glinda has summoned something unthinkable, the sky is red, the world is coming apart, and Leonard cannot win.

## The wish to be noticed

The most revealing detail is Glinda’s motive. The diary says she wanted other witches to notice her. It does not absolve the catastrophe, but it makes it painfully human: an enormous cosmic mistake born from loneliness and the desire for recognition. Dorothy’s optional response is grief rather than simple blame, because she arrives too late to help and cannot even be sure what Glinda saw.

A skeleton near the diary carries the Ring of the Good Witch, which reduces mana costs. Like Marianna’s ring, it turns a lost person into a useful object—but here the object also functions as the closest thing the game gives us to a grave.
