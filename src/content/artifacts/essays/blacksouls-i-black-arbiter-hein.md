# Black Arbiter Hein

Hein is one of the Black Trial figures whose apparent routine presence becomes a warning about the Black Court. The source guide follows him through domestic and hotel scenes before documenting the named Black Conductor encounter and its escalating dialogue.

## Recurrence and trial

His path is a useful reminder that *BLACKSOULS I* treats repeated NPC meetings as route information, not merely flavor. The final images frame the fight as a formal trial rather than an isolated boss room. Route and adult-content note: this guide record contains combat-route material; mature themes occur elsewhere in *BLACKSOULS*.
