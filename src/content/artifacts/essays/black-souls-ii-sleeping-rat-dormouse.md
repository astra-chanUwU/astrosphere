# Sleeping Rat Dormouse

Sleeping Rat Dormouse September 15, 2024 By Nate River BLACKSOULS BS2 Entities Guide Updated: February 4, 2025 Home BLACKSOULS Sleeping Rat Dormouse Sleeping Rat Dormouse is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 you can find at the Endless Tea Party area. She's at the same table and sitting next to March Hare Haigha and Hatter Hatta . Dormouse is a sleepy rat-girl. She's based on Dormouse which appears in Lewis Carroll's book &#8211; &#8220;Alice's Adventures in Wonderland&#8221;. Contents 1 Appearances | BS2 1.1 Endless Tea Party 1.2 Covenant with Hatta 1.3 Fighting Dormouse the Sleeping Rat 2 Personality 3 Design Appearances | BS2 Dormouse appears for the first time in the second BLACKSOULS game. She's at the Endless Tea Party, which you can reach after fully exploring Ox Ward University. Endless Tea Party At the Endless Tea Party, Dormouse will be &#8220;sitting&#8221; between Haigha and Hatta. She's not the best person to talk to since she's very sleepy and can barely speak properly. If you tell her you're going to Alice , she'll just start singing a lullaby. &#8220;Twinkle, twinkle, little bat, how I wonder what you're at&#8230; up above the world so high, like a tea-tray in the sky&#8230;&#8230; Twinkle, twinkle, twinkle, twinkle&#8230;&#8221; This lullaby is another reference to the &#8220;Alice in Wonderland&#8221; book. It is a verse recited by the Mad Hatter. It's a parody of &#8220;Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star&#8221;. If you try to send Dormouse to the dungeon, she won't resist at all. She'll stay unbothered and just continue sleeping. Covenant with Hatta You can't form a covenant with Dormouse, but you can form a covenant with Hatta which will also directly impact her. After you level up the covenant enough and talk to her, she'll tell you that you're &#8220;sort of better&#8221; when compared with Hatta and Haigha. She'll also insult their breasts, but then Hatta will just respond with &#8220;Yaay, go washboards!&#8221;, and Haigha will follow up by saying &#8220;Flatty-patty &lt;3&#8221;. You can also make love with Dormouse at this point, and this time she won't be sleeping &#8211; she's actually a passionate lover. Fighting Dormouse the Sleeping Rat If you come to the Endless Tea Party with low SEN, you will notice that everyone is out of their human forms, and instead, they're creepy monsters. When you try to kill Dormouse while she's in this form, you will initiate a fight with her where she'll be in a weird demon-like form. Hatta and Haigha will also come into the fight almost instantly, and then you will have to fight all three of them at the same time. Personality The only thing Dormouse cares about is sleeping. She's always very tired and deep asleep &#8211; she's so deep asleep that she sometimes may not notice very important things happening around her, or to her. She is sweet, but can sometimes be quite insane. Design Dormouse has short orange hair and dark eyes. She's pale and very small, and out of clothing her most notable accessory is her purple scarf she never takes off. The rest of her attire is cute and modest.
