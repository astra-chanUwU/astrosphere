# Caterpillar Shisha

Caterpillar Shisha debuts in *BLACKSOULS II* as a covenant character living at Caterpillar’s Spot beyond the maze-like passages of Spore Forest. Like much of the Wonderland cast, she is adapted from Lewis Carroll’s *Alice* books.

## Appearances | BS2

### Getting to Shisha

The repeating maze must be crossed in this order: **left, down, right, then up**. A wrong exit loops the passage. Forming Shisha’s covenant later unlocks immediate travel to her area.

### Meeting Shisha

Grimm finds Shisha lying on a mushroom and smoking her hookah. She initially ignores him, but calls him back when he tries to leave. She does not know where Alice is and redirects him to the Duchess. Before that route can be reached, Old Knight William must be defeated in Spore Forest.

### Friendship with Dodo

Shisha and Dodo know one another, although Dodo repeatedly pesters Shisha for knowledge and then forgets or misremembers what she was told. If Grimm has met Dodo while bound to Shisha, Shisha asks whether he has met “Walghvoghel,” the dodo’s original Dutch name, and complains about Dodo’s habit of asking questions without retaining the answers.

When both covenants are fully leveled, their pairing anniversary scene becomes available. Dodo boasts that Grimm is her best pupil and the two of them are the most beloved pair, making Shisha jealous. Grimm must choose which one he loves; choosing Shisha leaves Dodo furious.

### Covenant

Shisha’s covenant grants a defense increase and **Guard Effectiveness Up**. At level 1, Grimm can smoke with Shisha; an attempted kiss is rejected. At level 2, she allows the kiss even while protesting.

Each smoking scene raises the covenant, but the interaction can only be completed five times. If all five smokes are used and the covenant is fully leveled, Shisha later admits that she is tired and nearing the end of a life she says has lasted 21 million years. She asks Grimm either to leave her alone or to kill her. Returning to Caterpillar’s Spot after this conversation reveals her demonbeast form.

### Fighting Caterpillar Shisha

With 0 SEN, Grimm can attack Shisha immediately. Otherwise the five-smokes/full-covenant condition produces the later demonbeast battle. She is a strong two-phase opponent, changing from Caterpillar Shisha into **Imago Shisha** during the fight.

## Personality

Shisha is unusually relaxed for Wonderland. She spends her time smoking and resting, largely indifferent to her surroundings. Her restraint makes her feel less eccentric than many of the game’s other characters, though the Dodo scene exposes possessiveness beneath that calm exterior.

## Design

Shisha is a monster girl whose pale human torso, arms, and legs join a large cyan caterpillar body with additional caterpillar limbs. Yellow-green hair and dark-green eyes complete the palette. Her habitual expression is quiet and almost emotionless.
