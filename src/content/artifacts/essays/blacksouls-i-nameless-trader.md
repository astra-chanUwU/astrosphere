# Nameless Trader

Nameless Trader is Match Seller Elma’s father and a wandering merchant at Gothel’s Tower. He offers the Storm Ruler for 50,000 souls—his only significant sale, but a strong weapon for a player prepared to make the investment.

After the purchase, leaving him alive moves him to the Holy Forest. The guide also notes that he can be attacked immediately; the source gallery preserves his tower dialogue and the later Holy Forest appearances without treating that route as required.
