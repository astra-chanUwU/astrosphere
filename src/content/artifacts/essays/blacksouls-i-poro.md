# Poro

Poro is Red Hood’s talking wolf companion across the *BLACKSOULS* games. In *Red Hood’s Woods* he is a practical ally—merchant, weapon-reinforcer, and warning voice—but his loyalty also carries the violence of the story that made him.

> **Content warning:** Poro’s backstory includes child sexual abuse, murder, violence, and an adults-only route scene. This page documents the route context without reproducing explicit material.

## The wolf Red Hood chose

The guide’s key memory is brutally simple. Red Hood encounters a frightened wolf and, after he refuses to attack a human, feeds him her abusive grandfather’s flesh. The wolf names himself Poro. This is not a gentle version of Little Red Riding Hood; it is the series’ reversal of the tale, where the child has already learned how to be dangerous and the wolf becomes the one who stays.

## Loyalty with teeth

Poro remains at Red Hood’s side through the later games, including the developing *DEAD RED HOOD*. In *Red Hood’s Woods*, though, his appetite can overwhelm him. The fight is not a betrayal in the melodramatic sense; it is the story acknowledging that the protection built from violence cannot be cleanly separated from it.

Their reconciliation and adult scene belong behind the warning above. What matters for route reading is that Poro’s loyalty is never abstract: he protects Red Hood because he has seen what the world did to her, and because their bond was forged in a moment neither of them can make ordinary.
