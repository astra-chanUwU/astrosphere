# Divine Mistake Lilith

Divine Mistake Lilith is an optional opponent in the Succubus Church beneath the Old Rotten Burg bonfire. The church is crowded with succubi, so the guide recommends invisibility for a cleaner approach. Her dialogue carries lore context, while victory earns 98,000 souls and Lilith’s Heart.
