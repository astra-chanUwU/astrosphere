# Talking Rafflesia

Talking Rafflesia sits at the far-left side of the Finished Tea Party entrance to Alice’s Library. It is principally a lore encounter, connecting the Prayer Master, Alice, and the conspiracy around the Red Queen’s execution.

The flower has no elaborate combat route, so its value is narrative: stop and exhaust the conversation before treating the library approach as only a transit zone.
