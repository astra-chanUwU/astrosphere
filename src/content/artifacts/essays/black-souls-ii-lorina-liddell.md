- BS2

- DLC3

Lorina Liddell is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 that appears in the DLC3 part of the game during the visual novel section. She’s one of the three Liddell sisters, who are based on real-life sisters of real-life Alice Liddell.

Alice Liddell is the girl whose name Lewis Carroll gave to his Alice in Alice in Wonderland.

## Appearances | BS2

Lorina makes an appearance during the main part of the game, but the appearance in her real form takes place during DLC3.

### Winterbell Visual Novel

Upon further exploring the Winterbell area there will be a point where you will see Alice going somewhere. If you decide to follow Alice you will approach a door that leads to the visual novel section of the game, where you will be Lewis Carroll taking care of the three Liddell sisters – Alice, Lorina, and Edith.

The visual novel part starts with Lewis Carroll talking to Mr. Robinson, they will be talking about Alice, and then the scene will continue with a view of the residence area where the three sisters are, and where you will be spending time with them.

By proceeding further, you will see the Liddell sisters welcoming you.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/alice-welcomes-you-back.webp" alt="Alice welcomes you back" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Alice welcomes you back</figcaption></figure>

In the living room, Alice and Lorina will be together and you can talk to them for a bit. If you eat one of the sweets in the room Lorina will scold you for taking her sweets.

During the next day, Lorina will be in the living room with Edith, and they will be singing the Lion and the Unicorn nursery rhyme.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-singing.webp" alt="Lorina singing" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina singing</figcaption></figure>

When the night comes Edith will need some medicine, and by giving medicine to her you will be able to initiate an NSFW scene with Lorina later on. To give medicine to Edith you have to check on her, and then head straight towards the living room without checking anything else. Inspect the living room a few times, get the medicine, and give it to Edith.

The next morning Lorina will tell you to come to her room when night comes.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-s-invitation.webp" alt="Lorina's invitation" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina's invitation</figcaption></figure>

If you do come to her room she will appear to be salty over you helping Edith last night, Edith told her you were the one who came to her room to give her some medicine.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-thanks-you.webp" alt="Lorina thanks you" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina thanks you</figcaption></figure>

Lorina will thank you for taking care of her sister, and then she will reluctantly ask you if you want to sleep with her because she’s scared of sleeping alone. Choosing to sleep with her will initiate an NSFW scene with her.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-is-scared.webp" alt="Lorina is scared" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina is scared</figcaption></figure>

You will wake up the next morning next to Lorina, and she will ask you how long were you supposed to stay with them. Carroll will reply about how his leave ended yesterday, and Lorina will tell him to leave quickly.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-wakes-up-next-to-you.webp" alt="Lorina wakes up next to you" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina wakes up next to you</figcaption></figure>

When you get downstairs, the three Liddell sisters will be seeing you off, and that’s when you have the option to turn around to see them for the last time. When you turn around, you will only see puppets, and no one else.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/the-dolls.webp" alt="The dolls" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>The dolls</figcaption></figure>

#### Dead Lorina

If you don’t give medicine to Edith when she’s sick during the second night, Lorina will get mad that all three sisters aren’t together. She will postpone her tea party because of this.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-is-angry.webp" alt="Lorina is angry" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina is angry</figcaption></figure>

If Alice dies, when the 4th day comes, and if you go to the living room Edith will be waiting for you. If you ask her where is Lorina, she will tell you how she went to wake up Alice. Lorina will then instantly come back and say how there’s no Alice in the room, and Lorina will start questioning you because you must know where Alice is.

Carroll will respond with how she moved to a far far away place where she’s not coming back from, and Lorina will start calling him out for lying. Edith will tell Lorina to calm down, and then a black liquid will start coming out of Edith which will kill her, and totally shock Lorina.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-questions-you.webp" alt="Lorina questions you" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina questions you</figcaption></figure>

When you go to the vestibule, Lorina will be afraid you will kill her too. Carroll will try calming her down and then she’ll start questioning if all of this is just a dream, so to prove it’s a dream she will stab herself in the chest. Lorina will realize it’s not a dream because the stab wound hurts really badly, and then she will collapse and die.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lorina-liddell/lorina-kills-herself.webp" alt="Lorina kills herself" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lorina kills herself</figcaption></figure>

#### Aftermath

In the part of the visual novel section when everyone starts dying, and if you decide to go to Lorina’s room, you can see a queen piece lying on the bed by inspecting the room.

This references her new and refined appearance in the main game, where she takes the form of Queen of the Heart Lorina.

### Liddell’s Doll

When you enter the Mental Ward on the Receptionist’s table you can see a bunny doll close to the door leading further into the area.

By interacting with the doll it will just say “Liddell’s doll”. It appears to be a bunny plushie, and if your SEN is low there will be a knife in it.

This plushie looks like the one Lorina is holding during the NSFW scene with her, and there’s also that scene where Lorina uses a knife on herself in the visual novel section of the game.

## Personality

Lorina is quite assertive, she’s not weak like the youngest sister Edith, and she’s not as mischievous as Alice. She has a short temper and can get mad easily. Lorina is strict about maintaining a perfect picture of herself in front of Carroll and her younger sisters. She can be scary when she gets angry.

## Design

Lorina is quite petite. She has a little bit darker blue eyes and long blonde hair. She has the twin-tail hairstyle – her hair is held by two red ribbons. Her clothing is plain and simple, it just being a greyish light blue dress of some sort. Lorina’s facial expressions are stern – she often looks displeased.
