# Mock Turtle

- BS2

- Entities

- Guide

Mock Turtle is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 you can find inside the Sea Turtle Restaurant located at the Beach of Grief. She’s a lonely humanoid turtle and also the only worker in that restaurant, she has a close relationship with Griffy.

Her character is based on the Mock Turtle from Lewis Carroll’s book “Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland”.

## Appearances | BS2

Mock Turtle appears for the first time in the second game. She’s at the Beach of Grief area, inside the building at the upmost north point of the area – the Sea Turtle Restaurant.

Her nickname in the game is Mocky, it’s way easier to say that than her entire name at once.

### Sea Turtle Restaurant

You won’t be able to enter the restaurant right away because Mocky locked the doors. She’ll be flustered once you enter with the Master Key though. When you enter the restaurant, you can look around or go straight towards her to talk to her.

When you approach Mocky she will start panicking and she will even run away from you. If you talk to her, you can do a lot of stuff to her.

If you decide you want to calm her, just go along with her dialogue and let her take the medicine she needs. If you tell her you’re searching for Alice, she will say she doesn’t know who that is and will plead you to not hit her.

It’s easy to harm Mocky, but you can also get her covenant if you want to.

### Covenant

Mocky is one of the characters in the game you can form a covenant with. Her covenant is focused on increasing your MDF and Guard Effectiveness.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-mock-turtle/mocky-covenant.webp" alt="Mocky covenant" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Mocky covenant</figcaption></figure>

When you talk to her, she’ll start complaining about how someone spread rumors that her restaurant serves corpse meat, and now she has trouble with running the place because of that certain person.

But she’s also dead worried because someone during the night taps at the door, and sometimes she can feel someone staring at her through the windows, saying how it feels like someone is targeting her. That someone is most likely the Duchess because she confirms doing something like that during a conversation with Wolris.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-mock-turtle/duchess-stalking-mocky.webp" alt="Duchess stalking Mocky" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Duchess stalking Mocky</figcaption></figure>

When you bring her covenant to the first level, you can ask her to cook for you. This will allow you to buy certain consumables from her.

After a certain amount of leveling, you will be able to check what’s inside one of her closets. There you will find a swimsuit that you will make Mocky wear – you can initiate one of the NSFW scenes with her this way.

### Mocky and Griffy

Mocky is a nickname that Griffy uses frequently when addressing the Mock Turtle. The two of them are really close and are important to one another. Griffy frequents the Sea Turtle Restaurant from time to time and you can even find her there – they have a lot of interactions that are for now described in Griffy’s post in various sections, so if you’re interested in what’s going on between them check that out.

## Personality

Mocky is always very scared and very anxious. It’s easy to fluster her, but it’s also easy to make love to her considering she needs someone by her side – she’s very lonely. She’s sweet and cute, especially when she’s panicking – at times like that she needs medicine to calm down.

## Design

Mocky’s figure is small and cute. She’s always wearing a sweater with an opening which displays a little bit of her cleavage. Other than that, she has a very cute face, and she’s unable to hide her emotions at all which makes her reactions adorable.

Her eyes are bright green, and her hair is light blue, and it’s short. She even has quite visible ears and horns, although one horn is broken.
