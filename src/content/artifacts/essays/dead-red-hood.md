# DEAD RED HOOD: Agartha and the Black Trial

> **Adults-only content warning:** *DEAD RED HOOD* is an eroge. This guide discusses murder, prostitution, coercive choices, rape, nudity, and optional H scenes. Released adults-only CGs appear in a clearly marked gallery below.

*DEAD RED HOOD* is the announced fourth game in the *BLACKSOULS* series and the second title built around [Red Hood](/artifacts/blacksouls-i-red-hood). Unlike the earlier RPGs, it is being developed as a mission-based stealth-assassination game. No release date was listed when the source guide was last updated in May 2025, so every detail below should be read as development-era information rather than a promise about the final build.

## Underground City Agartha

The game moves beneath the surface to Agartha, a country transformed by the discovery of the steam engine. Its rapid industrial growth is paired with an increase in sinners and deadly crimes. Airships, bridges, wet alleys, machinery, and impossible creatures give the familiar fairy-tale horror a dense steampunk frame.

Red Hood works for the Black Trial, the executioner organization introduced in *BLACKSOULS*. Her assignment is to eliminate sinners quietly; completing that work offers her a chance at redemption. Poro remains beside her, tying this new premise directly to their longer history.

## Eight stealth missions

The announced structure contains eight stages. A mission is cleared by reaching a target, executing them, and escaping safely. Remaining unseen produces a higher score, although the source says score is not intended to alter the story. The developers have also discussed trophies as a possible source of replay value.

Targets draw on fairy tales, and assassinations are not confined to a single animation or weapon. The released sequence below presents the basic killing process as a deliberate chain: approach, control, execution, and departure.

## NPC choices and consequences

Agartha's inhabitants are not merely scenery. Red Hood can speak with NPCs or kill them, and the announced interaction set includes seduction, threats, theft, extracting information, forcing prostitution, and sexual assault. These systems make the player’s method part of the cruelty of the setting; their inclusion does not make the acts morally neutral.

## Optional H scenes and Poro's rescue

H scenes can be disabled. The developers also described a “Rape Cancel System”: if the player reaches an unwanted assault choice, a button summons [Poro](/artifacts/blacksouls-i-poro) to rescue Red Hood. The game is intended to be completable without viewing sexual content and without Red Hood having sex. This is an accessibility and consent-facing option inside a game that still depicts coercive material.

## Ten NPC states

NPC behavior is organized through ten visible states:

- **Normal:** neutral behavior.
- **Trust:** the NPC trusts Red Hood.
- **Distrust:** the NPC doubts her.
- **Hostile:** combat or escape becomes possible.
- **Lust:** sexual interest; cruel NPCs may attempt assault.
- **Fear:** caused by threats or approaching while bloodied.
- **Dying:** the NPC has suffered a fatal injury.
- **Unconscious:** the NPC has been incapacitated rather than killed.
- **Sleep:** the NPC is asleep.
- **Nihility:** unexplained. Its Japanese description invokes “Yog,” suggesting a possible connection to Yog-Sothoth and Mabel in *BLACKSOULS II*, though that remains speculation.
