# The Dog

The Dog is in the southwest of Rotten Burg. Petting him establishes the rescue route; after the Iron King falls, he leaves the Burg and can be found in the eastern Holy Forest. Killing him is possible, but closes that route immediately.

In the forest he has a random chance to bring Candy, and a rescued dog can appear at the Ending A celebration. It is a minor interaction with a welcome amount of persistence across the game’s otherwise brutal state changes.
