# Snow White’s Prince

Snow White’s Prince is a compact but hostile encounter in the Snow White portion of *BLACKSOULS I*. His dialogue and options establish the meeting before the route can turn to combat; the final frames document the fight and its death state.

As with many named NPCs, the useful advice is restraint: talk first, understand the branch, then commit only if the fight and its consequences fit the run.
