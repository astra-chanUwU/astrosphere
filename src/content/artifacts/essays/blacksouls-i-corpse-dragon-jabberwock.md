# Corpse Dragon Jabberwock

Corpse Dragon Jabberwock is one of BLACKSOULS II’s most elaborate route figures. The source gallery follows a covenant from first approach through danger, the Hidden Snow Garden, and the unstable futures shown by endings F and G.

> **Content warning:** Adults-only content warning: Jabberwock’s material includes sexual content, kidnapping, violence, coercive branches, and disturbing transformations. This page documents the complete source gallery without explicit prose.

## Appearances | RHW

The first-game Jabberwock appears as a recurring enemy in Red Hood’s Woods. The later Corpse Dragon is a related but distinct version of the character, built around the same *Jabberwocky* imagery.

## Appearances | BS1

### Meeting Jabberwock

After reaching Wonderland, enter the large light-blue tower at the northwest corner. Beyond it, at Riverside Bank, a bloodied corpse beside a tree can still speak. This headless corpse is Jabberwock, defeated in an earlier battle.

Her dialogue borrows heavily from Lewis Carroll’s poem, including the invented word “frabjous.” She speaks about her son searching for Alice and asks Grimm to return to her arms after defeating the force pulling the strings.

### Unlocking Ultimate Skills

The **Take her hand** option lets Grimm unlock an Ultimate Skill for a companion who has reached level 100. The service costs souls, and every companion has a different skill. These abilities are powerful combat upgrades.

## Misc | BS1

### Fighting Jabberwock

Trying to kill the corpse begins a battle that reveals her true form: a headless, one-armed demon-like creature carrying a scythe, with wings and a large tail. Victory rewards souls and an Ore Slab, after which the corpse disappears.

### The Crawling

After all companion Ultimate Skills are unlocked and Ending D is complete, Jabberwock speaks about the Prayer Master, the one who created Wonderland, and the feeling that something is wrong with the plan. The conversation breaks apart when she begins repeating that the Crawling has arrived.

## Appearances | BS2

The second game gives Jabberwock a new body and a much larger role. After defeating the Beast of Conceit, continue down the bridge into Riverside. The figure first appearing as Alice changes into Corpse Dragon Jabberwock, whom Grimm may choose to kill or spare.

### Covenant

Jabberwock’s covenant grants **Crit Chance +30%** and **Accuracy +30%**.

## Misc | BS2

### Fighting the Wicked Dragon Jabberwock

Killing her immediately begins a difficult battle against a high-health enemy with strong attacks. Victory grants a Tainted Black Soul and an Ore Slab, while Grimm loses 30 SEN and receives the Soul of the Jabberwock.

### Jabberwock Kidnaps You

After befriending Jabberwock, selecting the assault option can lead to a forced loss. Grimm survives but is locked in the basement beside the Carroll River bonfire. Jabberwock then discusses her history as a headless corpse, the old game, Alice, the Great Ones, and the cycles before the coercive adult route and an intervention.

### Jabberwock, Vorpal, and Vernai

The Vorpal Sword recalls the weapon that severed Jabberwock’s head in the poem and provokes a nostalgic reaction. Vernai can also be summoned for the fight. If Grimm loses while Vernai is present, the basement scene can lead to Vernai confronting Jabberwock and killing her to rescue him.

### Hidden Snow Garden (and Snowmen)

Jabberwock’s basement contains snowmen representing Mary Sue, Red Hood, Alice, and Grimm. With 0 SEN, other snowmen provide hints about escaping the cycles and avoiding the wrong prologue choice. With a high SEN and Jabberwock’s covenant, the Hidden Snow Garden becomes accessible.

Jabberwock calls it her workshop and admits that she no longer remembers why she makes snowmen. She asks Grimm whether he wants to be her child, then warns that the door ahead leads toward DLC3 and that the Chaos Dungeon should be conquered first.

### Fighting the Abominable Hunter

At 0 SEN, the regular encounter can be replaced by the Abominable Hunter, Jabberwock’s stronger nightmare form. Defeating it yields more souls.

### Endings F and G

After finding and defeating Meryphillia at least once, Grimm can encounter Jubjub, Bandersnatch, and Jabberwock in the Little Girl’s Nightmare. Staying to help Meryphillia leads to the Three Nightmares battle and, after their defeat, Jabberwock freezing and eliminating the other two when they refuse to continue.

Meryphillia identifies Jabberwock as belonging to the Cosmos<Heaven> race. Jabberwock rejects the Great Ones’ title and instead describes herself as a creator who wants to watch souls become truly black. She reveals that she helped shape the child’s hatred and then transforms from Corpse Dragon Jabberwock into Wicked Dragon Jabberwock for the final fight.

### Jabberwock and Mary Sue

Jabberwock’s story about a child starved of a mother’s love appears to point to Mary Sue and Baphomet. The Winterbell flashback visitor, who calls Mary her cute little Mary, supports the implication that Jabberwock and Mary have a prior connection.

### Mother Alice

Jabberwock is the true identity of Mother Alice, one of the three opening Alices. She is charming and affectionate toward Grimm but also possessive. During Ending H, Mother Alice and Daughter Alice try to stop Grimm from leaving Wonderland until Little Sister Alice intervenes and exposes their identities long enough for him to escape.

## H Scenes | BS2

Jabberwock has five H scenes. The covenant unlocks her service at level 2 and making love at level 3; additional branches cover rape, the dungeon version, and a reverse-rape scene after losing to her. The source guide records the poses, fetishes, and route conditions alongside the screenshots in this page’s gallery.

## Personality

Jabberwock is impulsive and unstable, but she also cares deeply about Grimm in a possessive, maternal way. She alternates between helpful guidance, threats, lust, and unsettling revelations about the Great Ones and the cycles. Her desire to create and observe black souls is central to her role.

## Design

Jabberwock is a beautiful but visibly rotten corpse with a curvy build, demon wings, a tail, and unusual limbs. Her white hair and single glowing blue eye give her a striking silhouette, while the headless corpse imagery connects her design to the Vorpal Sword and Carroll’s poem.
