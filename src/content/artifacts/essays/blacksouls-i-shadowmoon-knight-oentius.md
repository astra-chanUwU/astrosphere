# Shadowmoon Knight Oentius

Oentius waits in a tucked-away section of Subterranean Land K’n-Yan after the Lust Demon Satyr and the Entrance into Darkness. He is a hopeful Black Trial aspirant, which makes the later meeting with Baphomet bitter: she judges him impure and kills him before the trial can become what he imagined.

You can challenge him earlier, but he is a serious fight. The compensation is the Shadowmoon Blade, a weapon whose attack rises with sins; that makes Sinful Donell’s stock an obvious mechanical partner. It is a clean route trade-off: remove an NPC now for a strong build tool, or follow the story to its grim conclusion.
