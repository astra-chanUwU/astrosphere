# Bunny Girl Mary

> **Adults-only notice:** This guide may discuss explicit scenes, coercion, violence, and spoilers.

Bunny Girl Mary is an NPC in BLACKSOULS 2 you can find for the first time in the Charles Tavern located in Lutwidge Town. For a bunny, she has a lot of charm. She’s working as a waiter in the tavern but will lure you to come to meet her in exchange for some information on Alice.


She’s a sex worker in her spare time.


## Appearances | BS2


Mary is a minor character in BLACKSOULS 2 but she’s still quite interesting for many reasons.


### Meeting Mary


You’ll most likely stumble upon Mary for the first time at the Charles Tavern that’s located somewhere in Lutwidge Town. This tavern is full of interesting people but she stands out the most because she’s a pretty female serving drinks.


If your SEN is low, you won’t be able to talk to her. She’ll be walking around the area but the conversation won’t be happening.

### Meeting Up With Her


If you talk to Mary while she’s serving drinks in the Charles Tavern, and tell her that you’re looking for Alice, she’ll say she knows something about her and will redirect you to a certain location where she’ll tell you more about what she knows about Alice.


She’ll tell you to go to the hut in the Brothel Street area.


Brothel Street is a little bit far from the tavern, but once you get there, you will easily find it because there are little to no houses you can enter.


When you enter the Prostitute’s Hut she’ll come inside shortly after you.


She’ll give you a requirement you have to fulfill before she’s supposed to tell you more about Alice, and that requirement is to buy her services. When you finish with the intercourse, she will just tell you she doesn’t know anything about Alice, and will basically just use you for money and sex.

### Talking to Mary


You can talk to Mary ass she has some interesting things to say. She will talk about how the balance between men and women is perfect in Lutwidge Town, but there are some shady rumors spreading about Wonderland being ruined by the “excess of it”.


Mary says that the Queen of Hearts carries out executions daily and that the Slaughterhouse and the Mental Ward are used for locking people as well. She will then proceed to call out madmen who are “so obsessed with being seen as heroes by the people”, which is most likely a reference to Jack the Ripper.


From her dialogue, you can learn that another prostitute she raised is dead, and that makes her heart sick. She says she did too many inhuman things herself, but she at least wishes to find her lost younger sister before she dies – her younger sister is March Hare Haigha.


If you have a Bunny Suit you can give it to her but she will tell you to give it to someone else because she has plenty of them already. Mary will tell you she knows there are other women who would like to make you happy.

### Additional Scenes


When the cycle hits 5 Progression Points, Mary will offer you to try something new if you try to purchase her. Doing that will lead to some additional scenes that are from the third person.


### Death


If Jack the Ripper is still alive and you met Mary in the hut already, once you reach 4 Progression Points she will be killed in Upper Lutwidge. In her room is a letter from Detective Holmes, who’s saying that he found the whereabouts of Jack the Ripper so he’s requesting “Mrs. Hudson” to come to Upper Lutwidge tonight.


If you come to Upper Lutwidge in the top left part of the area you will find Mary’s dead body. Her abdomen is sliced open and by searching her body you can find a Bunny Headpiece.

## Personality


Mary’s personality isn’t anything too complex. She’s just a greedy bunny girl who also likes to have a lot of intercourse with others. Even though she’s poor, she’s still somewhat logical and stands her ground if you call her a whore.


She’s quite a passionate lover.


## Design


Mary is an anthropomorphic bunny. She has white skin (or fur) and a very developed body. Her clothing is not the most appropriate clothing out there – she wears fishnet tights that always stay on her, along with a perverse outfit.


Her face is smug, but also very attractive.
