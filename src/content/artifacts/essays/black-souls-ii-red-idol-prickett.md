# Red Idol Prickett

Red Idol Prickett September 21, 2024 By Nate River BLACKSOULS BS2 Entities Great Ones Guide H content Updated: May 4, 2025 Home BLACKSOULS Red Idol Prickett Red Idol Prickett is an idol and the second-generation Red Queen in BLACKSOULS 2. She can be found in Queensland, which is her garden . She's an important character in the overall story of the game. To get to Queensland you need to hop on a train at Abandoned Station Hanover. You can either show the Train Ticket to Hein , that's given by the Duchess , or you can just kill Hein if you're strong enough. Prickett's Concert Before you meet Prickett you should go to the Theater area inside the Queen's Castle because that's where you can witness Prickett perform as an idol. This is also where you will hear one of the very rare soundtracks in the game that has actual vocals, as Prickett will be happily singing for you and the other Followers of the Idol . To activate the concert you must have high SEN , you mustn't attack a Follower of the Idol, you shouldn't meet Prickett, and when you come into the Theater you should wait for a little bit before the concert starts. Prickett's Concert 1 of 3 Here are the lyrics of the song Star Dream Gazer, that Prickett is singing: &#8220;Hand outstretched, I make a wish Upon that sweet-sweet shooting star Running all across the night sky&#8221; &#8220;Past rainbow bridges, through cloud whales I want to send two handfuls of huge heart candies&#8221; &#8220;I'd rather dream, I want to feel them more, Every irreplaceable moment, I spend with you&#8221; &#8220;Laughing, crying, holding each other, always together We vowed to write a new page in our book&#8221; &#8220;Taking a detour side-by-side, to the next galaxy We'll ride on the orbit of Milky Way&#8221; Red Room One of the first places where you can meet Prickett is the Red Room, which is her room located at the back of the Theater. If you have 0 SEN and come into Prickett's room, you will see Alice instead of her for a brief second when you talk to her for the first time. Prickett will be surprised to see you because you’re basically in the no-entry area. She’ll ask you if you’re one of her fans. Although you can agree to that, you can try killing her or sending her to the dungeon if you don’t like her for some reason. She’ll then proceed to tell you how she originally lived in the cosmos, but she had to fall on this planet because of some stuff. Soon enough, you will get the option to ask Prickett where is Alice, but she will ask you if you really explored the entirety of Wonderland, saying how &#8220;it's a special theme park prepared with lots of effort just for you☆&#8221;. If you continue talking to her, she will tell you that there’s a girl waiting for you in the next room. She is most likely talking about Leaf who is now known in the game as Mary Ann because she's in the Fairy Jail inside the Queen's Castle. If you ask her where is Alice again later, she’ll tell you that Alice must have been changed for Mabel. Red Room 1 of 4 Prickett’s Bed If you come next to Prickett’s bed and interact with it, Prickett will make fun of how boys are always “mesmerized with beds”. Then she will ask you if you’re up for some fanservice because doing stuff with her fans is what gets her going. This will activate an NSFW scene on the bed. You can do this again at any time by interacting with the bed again. Fighting the Red Idol Prickett If you don’t like Prickett that much and just want to kill her as soon as possible, you can try but that will initiate a fight with her. She’s difficult to defeat because she has a lot of health, and her attacks are very strong. Prickett can basically oneshot you with just a single kick. Killing her will give you a Girl’s Scent and a Tainted Black Soul. You will also lose 30 SEN . Fighting the Red Idol Prickett 1 of 2 Covenant Prickett is one of the characters you can get in a covenant with. Level 0 &#8211; Cost : Free. Abilities : Chance to be targeted+200%. Options : X. Level 1 &#8211; Cost : 1000. Abilities : Chance to be targeted+300%. Options : Kiss. Level 2 &#8211; Cost : 3000. Abilities : Chance to be targeted+400%. Options : Kiss, I want you to service me. Level 3 &#8211; Cost : 7000. Abilities : Aggro+500%, Drop Rates Doubled. Options : Invite her on a date, Kiss, I want you to service me, Make love. When you're about to enter a covenant with Prickett, she will say: &#8220;You want to enter into a covenant with Prickett? Yup, sure thing☆. To be honest, I can feel the Love Destiny connecting me with you! I'll sing to spread the epidemic of smiles through the mirror～☆&#8221; After getting to the 3rd level of the covenant, when you talk to Prickett she will ask if you could take her on a date. If you tell her &#8220;What's this all about?&#8221;, Prickett will say that she has been secluding herself inside for so long, and that she's sick and tired of it. She will also mention that she's a girl and that there's nothing wrong with hoping to go on a date. If you tell her &#8220;No way&#8221;, Prickett will respond with: &#8220;Auuu～! Miiiser!&#8221; You will be able to invite Prickett on a date now. Level 3 dialogue : &#8220;(your name), your psyche is always sitting somewhere between mad and sad☆ Negative emotions are no good～! C'mon, smiiile☆&#8221; Date with Prickett If you maxxed out your covenant with Prickett, she will offer you to go on a date with her. She will ask you whether you want to go on a date with her or not, but it will be left to you to decide when you want to take her. When you call her on the date, it will start in the middle of Queensland, and it’s on you to take her wherever you want. You can get some food with Prickett at Vauxhall Stall , watch Punch&Judy with her, go to a Mystery Maze with her, go to the Lovecraft Cinema with her, and buy candy with her. Prickett Date 1 of 4 If you press the “A” keyboard button, you will be able to do certain actions lovers probably do on dates. Leaving the Queensland area isn’t possible, you will be given the option to end the date. Eating With Prickett You can go and eat with Prickett if you go to the Vauxhall Dealer. You can order a Snap-dragon-fly, Bread-and-Butterfly, Yogurt Sauce, Rato-Potato, or Tstogu Milk. Prickett will order one glass of Tstogu Milk and she will happily drink the weird drink. You will enjoy the peaceful time with her but suddenly you will also question whether you should be doing this or not because you already have your dear Alice, and then Grimm will say &#8220;The stars in Prickett's eyes twinkled as if she knew what was going on behind my dejected expression.&#8221; Prickett will say how you and her are quite similar, describing the both of you as &#8220;souls existing in abundance, scrambling for any sort of control, and trying to satisfy uncontainable desires&#8230;&#8221; She will ask if it feels constraining, asking why not sneak away just like she did. If you say &#8220;I don't know what you mean&#8221; she will say that it's nothing complicated and that she's only talking about just you two running away from this land. If you say &#8220;I have to search for Alice&#8221; she will say that's too bad, and that Prickett can never become Alice or Mabel. If you say &#8220;One day time to separate will come&#8221; she will ask if you will come to visit her again, when that one day comes. When the conversation ends she will apologize for &#8220;this lame talk&#8221;, and say &#8220;I was running a little low on spices, and, to you, I still&#8230;&#8230;&#8221;, but before she finishes her sentence she will stop herself from saying anything and will just empty her glass of Tstogu milk. Prickett Starts Crying Prickett will start crying after the Tea Party for Three movie ends in Lovecraft Cinema while you're on a date with her. This doesn't happen to her in any other movies. When you ask her why is she crying she will say how that's strange and that maybe the movie was just that sad. During this moment you can clasp her hand but you can also ignore her. The movie Tea Party for Three is about the Prayer Master , Alice, and the Nameless newcomer, and their fun time in Wonderland. The Newcomer and Alice fell in love with one another while the Prayer Master got jealous of that love and therefore prayed for them to be separated. More details about the movie can be found in the Prayer Master's post. Mystery Maze Alice Inside the Mystery Maze, Prickett will separate herself from you and you will have to search for her inside the maze. There’s a random chance you will find Alice in the maze, but you will not be able to understand her without Prickett’s Ring. Then, Prickett will appear instead of her, with quite depressing things to say to you, most importantly about how she’ll be left alone once you find Alice. Alice and Prickett 1 of 6 The encrypted dialogue isn’t anything special, besides her just screaming “no”, and to &#8220;not look here&#8221;. Prickett’s Ring When you finish your first date with Prickett, and talk to her again in her room, she will give you her ring – the Prickett’s Ring. This ring will allow you to understand what certain characters are saying when their text suddenly becomes cryptic. This ring is important for understanding certain dialogue important for the game’s lore, and it's a must-have for Ending H. Prickett's Ring 1 of 3 Prickett’s Tragedy In the Winterbell area, there are some houses you can enter, and inside those houses, there are stuffed white bear plushies that hold memories of certain events, that happened between certain Great Ones. This one memory starts with a description of a red room, this red room has red carpets, red wallpapers, red desks, and scattered deep red blood. Then the scene will cut to sharp knife stabs in the abdomen, chest, and neck, and it will continue with a description of wounds opening and closing, and the pool of blood constantly spreading. Then Prickett will utter some sounds, and the sounds of knives and knife stabs will continue happening. The narrator will describe how those aren’t tears of pain, but rather of sorrow, saying how the room will turn into a pond because of all the blood and tears. Prickett will be constantly apologizing, and just right when she was about to sever her neck, a white hand stopped her from doing that. Node will say that she can’t watch this anymore, and will ask Prickett why’s she trying to kill herself. Node stopped herself from saying this out loud because she didn’t want to scold Prickett because according to her she was in a seat from where she couldn't be blamed.
