# BLACKSOULS I: The First Descent

*BLACKSOULS I* is a dark fantasy JRPG built from a collision of fairy tales, horror, and Souls-like pressure. It begins from a deliberately disorienting place—a prison, an escape, a powerful ally overwhelmed by a dragon, then a forest that feels less like safety than a question. The point is not a clean retelling of a familiar story. The game borrows those stories because readers already bring expectations to them, then makes those expectations unreliable.

Its world moves between named places such as Holy Forest, Rotten Burg, Skeleton Maze, Snowy Garden, and Gothel Tower. Those names make the setting legible at a glance, while the imagery keeps pushing it toward decay, dread, and strange beauty. The result is a fairy tale that has not merely become darker; it has acquired rules, enemies, systems, and a history the player has to uncover.

## A cast of recognisable strangers

Alice, Dorothy, Fairy Leaf, and the Black Goat show the game’s basic trick. They are familiar names or shapes, but their presence is not a promise that the player already understands them. In *BLACKSOULS I*, recognition is the beginning of a question: what has this particular world done with the character, and what will it ask in return?

That is why the character list makes a better next stop than a lore dump. Companions, princesses, Black Trial figures, NPCs, and enemies overlap in ways that make the world feel inhabited rather than neatly sorted.
