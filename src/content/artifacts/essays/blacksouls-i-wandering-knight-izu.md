# Wandering Knight Izu

Izu, prince of the Lost Empire and Cinderella’s son, is a multi-stop route that can disappear if its timing is missed. Meet him at Fort Ivern before defeating the Knight in Armor, then save him in the Graveyard Town Elixir graveyard before Patrasche’s Cathedral state removes him. After the four Demonic Princesses fall, he moves to the Holy Forest; entering the Lost Empire too early also loses that conversation.

His final appearance is before the Ash-Clad Stairway. Talking there unlocks his appearance in the Room of Reminiscence. Wearing it lets the player approach Cinderella without an immediate fight and enter her covenant. **Adults-only route note:** the covenant can lead to a consensual intimate scene; that is route context, not a combat reward. Killing Izu prevents the appearance/covenant branch.
