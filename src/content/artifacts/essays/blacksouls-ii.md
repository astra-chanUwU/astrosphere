# BLACKSOULS II

*BLACKSOULS II* is a souls-like JRPG eroge made by the doujin circle Eeny, meeny, miny, moe?. It is the sequel to *BLACKSOULS I* and takes place afterward in a fantasy world populated by frightening creatures, spirits, and fairy tales.

Toro is credited with the story and all CG artwork. The game is available from its [DLsite release page](https://www.dlsite.com/maniax/work/=/product_id/RJ237469.html).

Compared with the first game, the sequel significantly expands both story and gameplay. It is harder, takes more effort to finish—and considerably more effort to complete—and includes numerous unlockable NSFW character scenes.

## Setting

The new fantasy world is much larger than the one in the first game and noticeably darker. It offers more places to explore, weapons to discover, and character storylines to follow.

Unless the prologue is skipped, the player first chooses among Daughter Alice, Little Sister Alice, and Mother Alice. Each Alice has unique opening scenes, but the arrangement is short-lived: the world begins collapsing, leaving the protagonist alone in a mysterious area and forcing him to begin an adventure through Wonderland.

The source galleries show Alice and the three DLC releases, followed by Library Dream, Pond of Bloody Tears, Lutwidge Town, Liddell Cemetery, Endless Tea Party, and the three opening Alices.
