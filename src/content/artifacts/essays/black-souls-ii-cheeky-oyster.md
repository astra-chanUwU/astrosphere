# Cheeky Oyster

> **Adults-only notice:** This guide discusses adult routes, coercive teasing, violence, and spoilers. Explicit scenes are not reproduced here.

Cheeky Oyster is one of the few surviving oysters in *BLACKSOULS II*’s Oysters’ Rotted Sea. She is a bratty, annoying Great One and Kuti’s sister. Her small shell-bound body hides a character who is much stronger and stranger than her teasing personality initially suggests.

## Appearances | BLACKSOULS II

Cheeky Oyster appears for the first time in the second game, in a secluded part of the Oysters’ Rotted Sea.

## Cheeky Oyster’s Quests

Grimm must explore the Oysters’ Rotted Sea to find Cheeky Oyster. She sits out in the open in a secluded section, waiting for someone to approach. When Grimm talks to her, she makes fun of him but offers a reward if he helps her with a small task.

She gives Grimm 99 Polluted Fish and asks him to distribute them to other people. The absurd quantity can be cleared by placing the fish in Polar Bear’s storage. When Grimm returns, Cheeky Oyster explains that eating the fish turns people into fish-like creatures. She wanted as many people as possible to eat them because the task was a prank.

The reward is a Shell Shield. Grimm automatically protests because the reward is poor, and Cheeky Oyster teases him about wanting something naughty and enjoying girls stepping on him. She then gives Grimm the task of killing Wolris. After Wolris is dealt with, Grimm can finally access the adult route with Cheeky Oyster.

## Fighting Zoa, the Princess of R’lyeh

If Grimm tries to kill Cheeky Oyster, she reveals her true form and becomes Zoa, the Princess of R’lyeh. The fight can be difficult because Zoa has a large amount of health and powerful attacks. Her strength also explains why Wolris did not simply eat her despite Cheeky Oyster’s small and vulnerable appearance.

Killing Zoa awards a large number of souls, the Wand of R’lyeh, and a Tainted Black Soul.

## Zoa Takes Over Wolris

When Jubjub’s Madness event occurs, Wolris can go to Zoa and try to eat her. Both Wolris and Zoa must still be alive for this event to happen. Grimm must go and fight Wolris after she reveals her true form when the event is triggered.

The fight begins with Wolris complaining to Carpenter that this time she roasted her meal instead of eating it raw. Her stomach begins to hurt, and she suffers from what she has eaten. Zoa, the Princess of R’lyeh, then appears in Wolris’s place. Zoa’s language is vulgar, and she is furious that Wolris ate her.

## Personality

Cheeky Oyster is a brat. She enjoys teasing, and her naturally dominant personality makes the behavior feel effortless rather than performed. She often speaks negatively about her little sister Kuti, but she does love her; insulting and making fun of people is simply how Cheeky Oyster expresses herself.

She is also lively. The energy of her dialogue contrasts with the broader horror of the Oysters’ Rotted Sea, where survival itself is unusual and the Great Ones’ bodies conceal more dangerous forms.

## Design

Cheeky Oyster is always inside her shell. Her body and everything around it are white and slimy, and she is very small. Her hair appears short, her eyes are darker, and her smile is permanently bratty. Her facial expressions are unusually vivid, letting the character’s teasing mood remain visible even when the setting becomes grotesque.
