# Tawny and EFChat

![Tawny](/media/people/tawny/tawny-pfp-x.jpg)

The web does not need every conversation to become a record.

[EFChat](https://efchat.net/) is a small chat app made by [Tawny](https://x.com/tawnniee), organized around named Spaces. A Space can be public or private; its real value is the context it creates while people are there. When the conversation ends, it does not need to be turned into a searchable feed, a personal brand, or a permanent piece of content.

That is a useful counterweight to the archive instinct behind AstroSphere. Some things should be preserved carefully: essays, images, links, reading trails, and the places worth revisiting. But the talk around those things can stay light. It can be immediate, unserious, or private. It can disappear without being treated as a failure.

## A room is the point

EFChat calls its rooms Spaces, which gets the design idea exactly right. The room is not merely a container for messages. A name, a subject, and the people who arrive give it a temporary character. You can make a Space for a game, an anime, a late-night thought, or a group of friends, without needing to build an audience around it.

![EFChat logo](/media/projects/efchat/efchat-logo.webp)

This is closer to IRC, small forums, and the older social web than to a timeline. The interesting unit is a place with a reason to exist, not an endless stream competing for attention.

AstroSphere has its own public Space at [efchat.net/AstroSphere](https://efchat.net/AstroSphere). It is the ambient side of the project: a room for what is happening now, while the archive keeps what is worth returning to later.

For more, visit [EFChat](https://efchat.net/) or follow [Tawny on X](https://x.com/tawnniee).
