# White Crow

White Crow exchanges Candy for powerful equipment, supplies, and spells. In *BLACKSOULS I* it lives in the Backyard behind Red Hood’s House; killing Red Hood removes the merchant. In *BLACKSOULS II* it moves to the eastern Dungeon and follows the same concise trading role with a different inventory.

The first-game shop includes Divine Blessing, Ore Slab, costume items, the Shadowmoon Blade, and several rings. The sequel shop pivots toward keys, shards, Goddess’ Blood, Demonedge, and high-cost sorceries. Save Candy deliberately and check the screenshots before spending it on a lower-priority item.
