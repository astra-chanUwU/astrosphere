# Soul Lady Elisabeth

Soul Lady Elisabeth enters as an elegant, difficult presence whose lost ring turns a brief meeting into a test of trust. Her route expands from the companion structure into demonbeast encounters and the Red Hood material of BLACKSOULS II.

> **Content warning:** Adults-only content warning: Elisabeth’s route includes sexual content, coercion, violent transformation, torture imagery, and death. The gallery preserves documentation, not explicit prose.

## The ring and its bargain

The first meeting, ring search, location, and reward images make the quest unusually concrete: the player can see the object and the decisions that surround returning it. Hotel Poseidon and demonbeast scenes then show how little a polite bargain guarantees.
