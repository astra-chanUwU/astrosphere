# Blacksmith Lops

Blacksmith Lops waits at the lowest point of the Path to the Lower Layer. He is the area’s weapon-upgrade NPC: bring the appropriate ore shards and he will reinforce the player’s equipment. The guide’s small exchange makes the practical role clear—Lops has little to say, but he is an important stop when a weapon needs improvement.
