# All Characters in BLACKSOULS I

This visual index retains the supplied guide’s six categories, character order, and all 65 unique portraits. Five portraits recur under multiple roles in the original guide; their later entries remain in place below without duplicating the image file.

*BLACKSOULS I* is an adults-only eroge. H-scenes are part of the game’s wider character and route material, but this index remains non-explicit: it documents the cast without turning character thumbnails into an H-scene gallery.
