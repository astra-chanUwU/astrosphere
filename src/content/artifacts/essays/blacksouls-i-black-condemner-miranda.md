# Black Condemner Miranda

Miranda is one of the first BLACKSOULS I characters who makes the game’s idea of strength feel personal. She is a Black Trial executioner, a formidable fighter, and a companion who can follow the player through the game. Her route is easy to miss, but its shape is memorable: a hunt in the Tainted Bog, a choice about what kind of relationship strength creates, and a series of later scenes that keep testing whether Miranda is a weapon, an ally, or a person allowed to want something else.

## Appearances in BLACKSOULS I

Miranda’s first appearance is conditional. With five or more sins, she appears in the Tainted Bog and begins chasing the player in the same broad pattern associated with Lindamea. She is below the fog leading toward the Lust Demon Satyr encounter.

## Choosing Miranda as a companion

The companion choice sends Miranda to the Holy Forest. She introduces herself as a former member of the Black Trial and explains her attraction to strength in blunt, almost contractual language. She has spent years killing and searching for an opponent who could finally challenge her. The alliance is not sentimental at first. It is a pact between two people who recognise power in each other.

In the Holy Forest she waits in the eastern area, near the same part of the map where Elma and Goose can be found. Her offer is characteristically direct: if someone needs to die, tell her. The route also makes an important boundary visible. When the player attempts sexual assault there, Miranda rejects the act and says she would rather kill than copulate. That refusal matters because later scenes branch differently; Miranda is not written as having one fixed response to intimacy.

## Hotel Poseidon

At level 100 or higher, Miranda can be taken to Hotel Poseidon. Pinocchio’s room interaction opens a private scene with two possible outcomes: intimacy or killing. It is a stark BLACKSOULS choice, placing affection and violence inside the same room and asking what the player thinks “having” a companion means.

## Playing in the water

At level 30 or higher, Miranda can be encountered at the beach area of the Holy Forest. She is relaxing in the water and invites the player to join her. The scene uses nakedness and desire as its opening language, but its most useful information is about the Black Trial.

Miranda describes the organisation as an executioner guild led by the archdemon Baphomet, with its base in the Tainted Bog. She was an orphan taken in by Baphomet, who trained her for decades after recognising her talent for fighting. Miranda’s explanation of strength is bleak: getting stronger became the proof that she existed. She joined the player because the Black Trial could no longer make her stronger, and because Baphomet treated her as a tool rather than a person.

The water scene therefore works as both an adult character event and a lore conversation. Its intimacy gives Miranda room to soften, while the discussion reveals the institution she came from.

## In the Basement

The Basement branch is deliberately darker. Miranda accepts whatever the player chooses to do to her there, including rape, framed by the game as the “privilege of the strong.” This is sexual violence, not a neutral romance route, and it belongs behind the adults-only warning rather than being presented as a normal companion reward.

## Endings

Miranda appears in Ending A, where she is seated at the table and speaks briefly about the food. Her quietness fits the character: she is present, loyal, and not especially interested in filling silence for its own sake.

Ending C gives Miranda one of her strongest scenes. If she is alive, she can intervene when Lindamea appears at the Gathering of Black Souls. Without a covenant with Baphomet, Lindamea calls Miranda a traitor and prepares to fight her; Miranda tells the player to hurry and saves them. With the covenant, the conversation shifts toward control, puppetry, and the question of whether Lindamea is acting by his own will.

The exchange expands the Black Trial beyond a simple enemy faction. Baphomet is dead, the organisation has been annihilated, and Lindamea recognises that he was made to dance like a puppet. Miranda survives the encounter but is wounded and unable to move. Her final image in the sequence is not triumph so much as endurance: she spent everything to reach the player and is still alive.

## Appearance in BLACKSOULS II

Miranda also appears in *BLACKSOULS II* in an unusual form. There she is also Meryphillia, a corpse-eating ghoul. A full treatment belongs on Meryphillia’s own page, but the connection is worth preserving here because it changes how Miranda’s identity carries between games.

## Personality

Miranda is cold, brave, and nearly emotionless on the surface. Fighting is her obsession and her vocabulary. She wants opponents strong enough to give her a real battle, and she follows the player because she believes they can continue that challenge.

The small flashes of softness are therefore important. A smile, wet hair brushed from her face, a quiet meal, or the willingness to stand between the player and Lindamea all reveal a person beneath the executioner role. Miranda’s route does not erase her violence. It shows how loyalty can grow inside it.

## Design

Miranda’s clothing is rough and held together by ropes, with a brown hood shaped like an animal’s head. Her short black hair, dark eyes, pale skin, and oversized axe make the silhouette immediately readable. The design is simple, but it has the same practical severity as her personality: no ornament is allowed to distract from the fighter.
