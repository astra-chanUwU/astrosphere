# Beloved White Dragon Shirasu

Shirasu is an optional dragon in the Secret Garden. The player can choose not to kill him, but defeating him is required for the Moonlight Greatsword. The long fight includes a defensive phase that prevents attacks, followed by the dragon using the sword himself at low health.

FGGuides also records a visually identical Beloved Dragon later in Red Hood’s Woods.
