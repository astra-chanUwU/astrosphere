# Rapunzel

Rapunzel presides over the Summit in *BLACKSOULS I*: an indulgent, lonely princess whose courtly pose conceals the same violence that marks the other Demonic Princesses. Her story branches around Behemoth, the Desert Rose, and the player’s decision to treat a covenant as an alliance or a prelude to a fight.

## Covenant, flower, and confrontation

The nonviolent route asks for the Phantom Rose in the Helsa Desert. Returning it establishes the covenant and grants Rapunzel’s Favor, while the Behemoth route changes the outcome of the tower. Choosing hostility turns her into a difficult boss with a more grotesque second form. Route and adult-content note: this gallery records the source guide’s combat and character material; some *BLACKSOULS* routes contain mature themes.
