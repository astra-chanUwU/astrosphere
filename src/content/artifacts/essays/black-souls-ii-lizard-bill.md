# Lizard Bill

- Covenant

Lizard Bill is a character in BLACKSOULS 2. He’s a humanoid lizard and the main worshipper of the Serpent God. Bill and the Serpent God make a cute pair of effeminate boys. He is based on Bill the Lizard from Lewis Carroll’s book – “Alice in Wonderland”.

## Appearances | BS2

Bill is somewhere inside the Ripon Grand Cathedral, but you can’t find him right away.

### Getting to Bill

You won’t be able to get to Bill right away because the entrance to his altar will be closed, and you will have to open it first. To do that, you have to go to the basement area of the Ripon Grand Cathedral and then go past the skeletons and the Hydra enemy roaming around.

They can easily kill you at the beginning of the game, so it’s just best to avoid them by going invisible or being very quick. Once you get to the end of the area, pull the lever, and the giant door in the main hall will open – that’s where you’ll find Bill.

### Meeting Bill

When you first come into the Serpent God’s Altar area, Bill will most likely be on the ladders trying to reach something. You can come up to him, and he will nonchalantly ask you if you could hold the ladder for just a moment.

You will then be given the choice to keep the ladder steady, kick away the ladder, or ignore him.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/bill-s-ladders.webp" alt="Bill's ladders" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Bill's ladders</figcaption></figure>

If you keep the ladder steady, Bill will thank you while doing his thing on the ladders, you will then be given the option to either just hold the ladder or peek under his skirt. Peeking under Bill’s skirt will reveal the beautiful view of his ass.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/lizard-bill-s-plump-ass.webp" alt="Lizard Bill's plump ass" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Lizard Bill's plump ass</figcaption></figure>

Bill will then be done with his thing, and he will thank you very much for helping him out. You two will then introduce yourselves, and he will ask if this is your first time at Ripon Grand Cathedral because he would be happy to teach you all about it.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/meeting-bill-1.webp" alt="Meeting Bill, 1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Meeting Bill, 1</figcaption></figure>

If you kick Bill’s ladder, he will scream and fall from the ladder, asking you what you were doing. You will then get the option to either kill him or deceive him.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/kicking-bill-s-ladders.webp" alt="Kicking Bill's ladders" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Kicking Bill's ladders</figcaption></figure>

If you choose to deceive him, you will basically tell him that your foot slipped and hit the ladder by accident. Bill will tell you to be careful, asking if you’ve been injured, he will give you a Blood Stanch.

You two will then introduce yourselves, and he will ask if this is your first time at Ripon Grand Cathedral because he would be happy to teach you all about it.

Ignoring Bill will leave him confused because he will be sure that someone was behind him.

### Talking to Bill

After the whole ordeal with the ladders is done, Bill will be at the altar, and you can talk to him there.

Bill will tell you that he shares the teachings of the Serpent God at this temple, and he will declare that he’s the “shrine maiden” there. Bill says that the Serpent God is much more benevolent than other gods and that those who believe are repaid generously.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/meeting-bill-2.webp" alt="Meeting Bill, 2" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Meeting Bill, 2</figcaption></figure>

Bill will ask if you would like to enter his faith too.

Bill says that the Wonderland is torn by constant strife and that it’s a turbulent age. So it would be for the best to find beliefs that will support you. He knows that it must be troubling to be asked about it so suddenly, so he’d like to discuss it with you more once you’ve sorted out your feelings.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/meeting-bill-3.webp" alt="Meeting Bill, 3" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Meeting Bill, 3</figcaption></figure>

### More Talking and Gate Pass

If you talk to Bill again, he will wish you a good evening and ask if you perhaps have had a change of heart.

You will then be able to tell Bill that you’re going to Alice, and he will think for a bit but will shyly say that he doesn’t know about her.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/meeting-bill-4.webp" alt="Meeting Bill, 4" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Meeting Bill, 4</figcaption></figure>

But, after thinking for a little bit more, Bill will say that the Queen of Hearts might know something. He will explain that the Queen of Hearts is the lady governing this country, so you might be able to meet her if you head to Red Castle Frissel.

Bill says that the Queen of Hearts is an awfully unreasonable and terrifying lady. Taken back by the fact that you still want to go, he will say that he understands, and then Bill will give you a Gate Pass, which will allow you to pass through the Gate Soldiers.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/meeting-bill-5.webp" alt="Meeting Bill, 5" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Meeting Bill, 5</figcaption></figure>

Passing through Gate Soldiers can be done with fighting, but this is a more gentle way of doing it. They keep you from reaching the Garden of the Heart that leads to the Queen of Hearts’ castle.

Bill says that the Gate Soldiers should let you through if you show them the Gate Pass. But, he also says that they “aren’t right in the head”, so he can’t guarantee anything. Before ending the conversation, Bill will tell you to think more about entering the faith, if possible.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/meeting-bill-6.webp" alt="Meeting Bill, 6" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Meeting Bill, 6</figcaption></figure>

### Covenant and Its Events

Bill is one of the characters you can get in a covenant with.

- Level 0 – Cost: Free. Abilities: MP+10% / MP Costs*1/2. Options: Buy Sorcery Tomes.

- Level 1 – Cost: 3000. Abilities: MP+20% / MP Costs*1/2. Options: Buy Sorcery Tomes, Kiss.

- Level 2 – Cost: 7000. Abilities: MP+30% / MP Costs*1/2. Options: Buy Sorcery Tomes, Kiss, I want you to service me.

- Level 3 – Cost: 10000. Abilities: MP+50% / MP Costs*1/2. Options: Buy Sorcery Tomes, Kiss, I want you to service me, Make love.

#### Blackwell’s Punishment

If you manage to get Blackwell into the Ripon Grand Cathedral, eventually, as the Progression Points get higher, she will go insane. If she disappears from the room in the cathedral where your bonfire is, she will be in the basement where the lever for opening the door to the Serpent God’s Altar is.

If you kill the demonbeast version of Blackwell, Bill will mention how “an old woman in a plague mask” broke into his bedding and tried to steal the blood of the Serpent God. That made Bill really scared, but then you will tell him how you killed her, and he will be relieved.

This conversation will only happen if the level of your covenant is 1 or higher.

#### Summoning the Serpent God

After having sex with Bill while your covenant with him is on the 3rd level, Bill will say that he thinks he has heard the voice of the Serepent God. Bill says that he communicated to him that he wishes to reward you shortly.

<figure><img src="/media/images/black-souls-ii-lizard-bill/summoning-the-serpent-god-1.webp" alt="Summoning the Serpent God, 1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>Summoning the Serpent God, 1</figcaption></figure>

This will make Bill very happy as your “ardent faith has finally been recognized”. Bill says that this is such a great honor and that until now, the Serpent God has appeared in person only to him.

He says that you too will be offered a gift of Serpent God’s blood – he’s very glad that all this happened.

The next time you come to the Serpent God’s Altar, he will be sitting on the altar, right behind Bill.

If you then talk to Bill, he will ask to confirm if you can see the Serpent God’s divine form, urging you to talk to him. You will then have to talk to the Serpent God, who has a quiet voice. But Bill says that the Serpent God is very shy, so his “divine voice” might be a bit hard to catch, so he will interpret until you get used to it.

### Bill and Griffy

Under certain conditions, there’s a possibility you can encounter Bill and Griffy interacting with each other at the Serpent God’s Altar.

They will have a friendly conversation with each other, discussing Bill’s potential future role as a juror. He has already done it once, and Griffy is expecting him to do it again, but he will respond that he doesn’t want to follow the cause of the Queen of Hearts and that Griffy already asked him to do this.

Then their conversation will end with Griffy commenting on how Bill looks like a girl, he will cut her off there and then acknowledge your presence, asking Griffy to leave.

If you broke his heart, and when you get into a fight with him while he’s a demonbeast, Bill will have an outburst of emotions directed at you. He will call you his puppy love, asking what should he do for you to return his love – he’s going insane.
