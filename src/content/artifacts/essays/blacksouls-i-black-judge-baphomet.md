# Black Judge Baphomet

Baphomet’s lair is staged as a courtroom, but her route is less a single judgment than a chain of bargains, requests, and escalating forms. The guide’s screenshots move from introductory dialogue to boss phases and later material that places the Black Judge inside the game’s broader ending logic.

## Judgment, requests, and forms

The encounter has several distinct states: first contact, battle, a second phase, and quest dialogue that changes the meaning of the confrontation.
