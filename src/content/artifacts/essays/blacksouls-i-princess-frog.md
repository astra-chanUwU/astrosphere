# Princess Frog

Princess Frog condenses a familiar transformation tale into one of *BLACKSOULS I*’s stranger encounters. The source route moves from a choice prompt to the fight and the deliberately ungainly frog form that follows the confrontation.

## Encounter record

The sequence is short but useful as a route checkpoint: dialogue choices establish the encounter, Dorothy’s response supplies context, and the later frames show the transformed outcome.
