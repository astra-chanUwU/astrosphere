# Red Hood’s Woods

*Red Hood’s Woods* is a JRPG eroge made by the doujin circle Eeny, meeny, miny, moe?. Red Hood explores dangerous realms and fights powerful fantasy creatures and bosses. It is the first game in the *BLACKSOULS* series and remains relevant to the later story.

Toro is credited with the story and all CG artwork. The game is available from its [DLsite release page](https://www.dlsite.com/maniax/work/=/product_id/RJ259845.html).

The game is short and relatively simple, though some bosses require grinding. It also contains numerous NSFW defeat scenes triggered by losing to particular enemies.

## Setting

The story most likely occurs before *BLACKSOULS I*. Red Hood travels through mirrors leading to separate realms, defeating a boss in each one to open the route forward.

Many of these locations appear again in *BLACKSOULS II*, and Red Hood is aware of the connection. The source gallery records the basement, foggy forest, lake, Mushroom Village, snowfield, and Red Hood’s home later in the game.
