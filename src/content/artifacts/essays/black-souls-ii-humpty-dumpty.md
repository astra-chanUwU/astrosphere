# Humpty Dumpty

- BS2

- Entities

- Guide

Humpty Dumpty is a character in BLACKSOULS 2 that appears in a certain part of the Carroll River area. He’s most likely based on Humpty Dumpty from the book “Through the Looking-Glass, and What Alice Found There” written by Lewis Carroll.

He likes to tease the player, and at one point he even gives birth to the Golden Chick, who’s a fairytale Goose That Laid Golden Eggs.

## Appearances | BS2

It’s easy to miss Humpty Dumpty during a playthrough.

### Meeting Humpty Dumpty

In the Carroll River, in the southwest part of the area, you will find a secluded part with Humpty Dumpty sitting on the wall.

When you choose to talk to Humpty Dumpty you can ask him why is he so angry or why is he so shaky, to the first question he will respond by saying how it’s very provoking to be called an egg, and to the second question he will respond by saying that his sense of balance is perfect, however, those sorts of things aren’t worth worrying about.

Humpty Dumpty will ask for your name and once you tell him your name he will say that it’s stupid enough, asking you what it means. Talking more to Humpty Dumpty will reveal his feelings of nervousness and uncertainty, but he will say that he’s not especially mad at you, but rather he’s feeling that way for an entirely different reason – the reason being that Humpty Dumpty is pregnant.

Humpty Dumpty will then tell you to take responsibility for the pregnancy, he will say that he’s making you his servant and that you should bring him souls.

### Giving Birth

Humpty Dumpty will give birth when he reaches level 99 or higher. You have to give him a lot of souls to reach that number, but once you do he will start giving birth.

When the process starts it will be very painful for Humpty Dumpty, he will talk about his belly tearing and how he’s dying. He will start cracking and that will lead to something hatching out of him because, in the end, he’s kinda an egg. Although, the difference between him and an egg is that he can speak and is a mysterious entity.

Humpty Dumpty will give birth to the Golden Chick, and she will use his corpse as a nest.

### Killing Humpty Dumpty

If you don’t like Humpty Dumpty, you can kill him. This will initiate a morbid scene where you throw him off the wall and make him fall straight to the ground. All that will be left of him is a pool of blood.

### Fighting Humpty Dumpty

If you have 0 SEN and you want to kill Humpty Dumpty, you will first have to defeat him in a fight. In his nightmare form Humpty Dumpty is a giant cracked demonic-looking skull with a large golden ball inside it. He is floating and has something that resembles limbs hanging from him.
