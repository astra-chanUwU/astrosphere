# Brutalizing Angel Lingeriena

> **Adults-only notice:** This guide discusses explicit routes, coercion, violence, and spoilers. Explicit scenes are not reproduced here.

Brutalizing Angel Lingeriena is a *BLACKSOULS II* character who appears during the DLC3 sections of the game. She presents herself as an angel, but is actually a demon of violence like Victoria—and Victoria’s younger sister. Her idea of salvation is inseparable from pain, killing, and the conviction that she alone can send people to Heaven.

## Appearances | BLACKSOULS II

Lingeriena appears throughout the Crimean Nursing Graveyard. Her scenes turn the graveyard’s medical and religious language into a performance: patients are “saved,” pain is called treatment, and an angel’s blessing can be indistinguishable from a fatal blow.

### Meeting Lingeriena

The first time Grimm sees Lingeriena, she is dancing on a pillar on one of the upper floors of the Crimean Nursing Graveyard. A corpse lies beside the pillar. If Grimm calls her out, she offers to save him. Choosing that offer makes her kick Grimm with enough force to send him into a wall and kill him. Rejecting the offer earns a blessing before she flies away.

### Lingeriena’s Piano Skills

The elevator can take Grimm to the third floor, where Lingeriena is in a room filled with blood and bathtubs. She plays the piano on top of a bloody waterfall. Her singing is not especially good, but when she finishes, patients fall from the sky and more people are “saved.”

Lingeriena does not recognize Grimm when he talks to her there. She describes a curing method based on inflicting pain and killing people, then says that Grimm must also want to go to Heaven. She is too busy to kill him yet, which is the reason he remains alive.

As compensation for her wrongdoings, she gives Grimm the Fake Angel’s Wreath. Equipping it grants HP +30% and SPD +50%. She also says that there is a fake angel somewhere in the Garden and asks Grimm to call her if he sees it. Lingeriena cannot be killed or sent to the dungeon from this position because Grimm cannot reach her.

### After Defeating Florence

After Florence is defeated, Lingeriena can be found walking in the room below Florence’s room, where Nurse Call is located. She says that people who outlived her beg in tears to be saved whenever she walks as an angel. She enjoys the “lovely feeling” of saving them.

If Florence is saved, Lingeriena calls the “fake angel” gone. Florence was only human, she says, and therefore could not bring anyone proper salvation. Lingeriena doubts that Florence could ever have become the lord of the Garden.

Grimm can kill Lingeriena or send her to the dungeon, and either choice initiates a fight. When defeated, Lingeriena begs for mercy, but Grimm remains focused on Alice. The route then offers a choice between killing Lingeriena and taking her into an adult service scene. Afterward, Grimm hesitates over whether to kill her because her violence never actually saved anyone.

During the continuation of the scene, Grimm attempts to choke Lingeriena, but a spear pierces his brain. Lingeriena’s sister Courtisane arrives to save her. Courtisane is disappointed, though she does not scold Lingeriena severely. When Courtisane notices Grimm is still alive, Lingeriena tells her that he is dead, and the sisters leave together.

#### Lingeriena Kills Florence

If Lingeriena is left alone after Florence is defeated—without killing or saving Lingeriena—she can kill Florence herself. Under the required conditions, Lingeriena appears beside Florence’s blood-covered bed and wonders how “that thing” could ever have been an angel.

She calls Florence hairy and wingless and points out that Florence does not even have a halo. Florence is the fake angel Lingeriena keeps mentioning: Lingeriena decided to “save” her because she looked close to death.

### Fighting the Brutalizing Angel Lingeriena

Once Lingeriena can be found on the ground, Grimm can choose to fight her. She is a difficult enemy who repeatedly insists that Grimm needs to be saved and remains reluctant to let him go.

The battle has a second phase in which she becomes Violence Demon Lingeriena. This form is tougher and more frightening than the false angel. Killing her awards a Tainted Black Soul and several Black Souls.

If Grimm is in a covenant with Victoria, Lingeriena recognizes him as belonging to her elder sister’s household. She says she would have saved him immediately if he had mentioned that connection and declares that Victoria now owes her one.

## Personality

Lingeriena’s personality is deeply twisted. She believes she is an angel who saves people, but her “salvation” is mass murder carried out through the Crimean Nursing Graveyard. The angel persona gives her violence a moral vocabulary: pain becomes treatment, corpses become failed patients, and killing becomes a route to Heaven.

She tries to dress and behave like an angel, but the costume cannot soften the consequences of her actions. The source guide also describes her as a passionate lover, which makes her contradiction sharper rather than absolving her: tenderness and brutality occupy the same role.

## Design

Lingeriena initially resembles a conventional angel, but that appearance is a cover for her true nature as a violence demon. Her halo is fake. Feathers are attached to demonic wings, and a devilish imp tail remains visible beneath the angelic presentation.

Her hair is short, colorful, and carefully styled. Her face stays bright and cheerful, and her physical attractiveness is part of the disguise: Lingeriena looks like someone who should offer rescue, even while the graveyard reveals what her rescue actually means.
