# Sinner Wilt

Wilt appears outside Dorothy’s house after candy reaches Hansel. Do not collect the breadcrumbs beforehand: that state changes the sibling situation into immediate demonbeast trouble. Wilt claims to be their brother, fears the Black Trial, and offers souls for an Elma Match so the starving children can be fed.

Following the scene into the house reveals the route’s trap. The children do not recognise him, Wilt transforms, and the player must either let them die or fight. He is dangerous before and after the transformation, so treat this as a route encounter rather than a harmless dialogue branch.
