*Sorry, but I'm Not into Yuri* is an ongoing manga by Mochi Au Lait. It follows Itsumi Toudou, whose attempt to win over her homeroom teacher leaves her dealing with the sudden affection of Yomotsuka, the student committee president.

This archive entry is intended for mature readers and includes erotic content and sexual violence.
