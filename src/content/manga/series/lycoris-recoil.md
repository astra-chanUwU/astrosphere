*Lycoris Recoil* is an ongoing action-and-adventure manga adaptation by Spider Lily and Yasunori Bizen, based on the original television anime created by Spider Lily, Asaura, and Imigimuru. The official anime site identifies Spider Lily as the original work, Imigimuru as character designer, and A-1 Pictures as the animation studio. Official character and key-visual artwork is included for reference and commentary.
