description: "In a world where everyone takes wonders like magic spells and dragons for granted, Coco is a girl with a simple dream: she wants to be a wizard. After secretly watching him perform magic in a way she's never seen before, Coco soon learns what everybody knows might not be the truth and discovers that her magical dream may not be as far away as it seems... Winner of the 2020 Eisner Award for Best U.S. Edition of International Material—Asia and the Best Manga award at the 2020 Harvey Awards."
