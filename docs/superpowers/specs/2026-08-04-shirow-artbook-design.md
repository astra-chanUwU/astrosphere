# Shirow Masamune Artbook Feature Design

Publish a feature essay for *Shirow Masamune Artworks in the Shell*, the official 2025 exhibition artbook. The article belongs to the Anime and Games spheres, uses the supplied blue Motoko illustration as hero art, and presents the supplied Shirow artwork as a credited local gallery.

The article reports only the official announcement: the April 2025 exhibition, 260+ selected colour illustrations spanning 40+ years, A4-variant softcover, 304 pages, ¥6,000 before tax, and an April 11, 2025 release. It links directly to the official Ghost in the Shell announcement and labels all supplied art as third-party material used for identification and critical commentary.
