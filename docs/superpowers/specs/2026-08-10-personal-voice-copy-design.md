# Personal Voice Copy Design

## Goal

Make AstroSphere feel like Astro Chan's personal corner of the internet rather than an abstract archive product or an early concept document.

## Voice

Keep the writing atmospheric and indirect, with Astro Chan present through the framing and the specificity of the material rather than through a full biography. Use a small amount of first person where it feels natural, avoid corporate or AI-shaped language, and let the playful phrase "A personal collection of strange kawaii lil things UwU." establish the site's personality.

## Copy direction

- Home hero: "A personal collection of strange kawaii lil things UwU."
- Home supporting line: "A growing pile of manga, games, images, essays, research, and other things from my orbit."
- About: describe the site as Astro Chan's corner of the internet and name the existing content types and the imperfect, collected nature of the archive.
- Colophon: retain the useful technical facts, but explain the site's structure as a personal choice rather than a product specification.
- Contact: replace the placeholder with a warm invitation to say hello, share a connection, or discuss a project.
- Support: explain that support helps keep a personal site online and gives the work more time, while preserving the existing wallet and safety mechanics.
- Footer and metadata: identify the site as Astro Chan's personal collection and mention the actual content already present.

## Scope

Copy-only changes in the existing content and presentation files. No navigation, content model, support behavior, layout, or data changes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. No affected page describes AstroSphere as a generic archive without connecting it to Astro Chan or the actual material on the site.
2. The contact placeholder is removed.
3. Existing support wallet, copy, and supporter-list behavior remains unchanged.
4. Production build succeeds.
